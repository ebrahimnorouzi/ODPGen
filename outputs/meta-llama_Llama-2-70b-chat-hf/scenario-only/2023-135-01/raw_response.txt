Here is the OWL ontology design pattern (ODP) in Turtle syntax for the scenario of an author adopting different names to publish books, taking into account name changes due to marriage or other reasons:
```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#>.
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#>.
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>.
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#>.
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#>.

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
rdfs:label "Author Name Change Ontology" ;
rdfs:comment "An ontology for modeling author name changes, including adoption of different names for book publication and name changes due to marriage or other reasons.".

:Author a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Author" ;
rdfs:comment "An individual who has written a book or other written work.".

:Book a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Book" ;
rdfs:comment "A written work published by an author.".

:Pseudonym a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Pseudonym" ;
rdfs:comment "A name adopted by an author for publication purposes.".

:NameChange a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "NameChange" ;
rdfs:comment "A change of name by an author, such as due to marriage or other reasons.".

:AuthorName a owl:ObjectProperty ;
rdfs:label "author name" ;
rdfs:comment "The name of an author.".

:BookTitle a owl:ObjectProperty ;
rdfs:label "book title" ;
rdfs:comment "The title of a book.".

:PseudonymOf a owl:ObjectProperty ;
rdfs:label "pseudonym of" ;
rdfs:comment "The pseudonym adopted by an author.".

:NameChangeDate a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
rdfs:label "name change date" ;
rdfs:comment "The date of a name change by an author.".

:Author subClassOf :Person.
:Book subClassOf :WrittenWork.
:Pseudonym subClassOf :Name.
:NameChange subClassOf :Event.

:AuthorName domain :Author.
:AuthorName range :String.

:BookTitle domain :Book.
:BookTitle range :String.

:PseudonymOf domain :Author.
:PseudonymOf range :Pseudonym.

:NameChangeDate domain :NameChange.
:NameChangeDate range :Date.

:Author, :Book, :Pseudonym, :NameChange rdfs:disjointWith :Person.
```

Intent:
The ontology models the relationships between an author, their books, their pseudonyms, and any name changes they may have undergone, including those due to marriage or other reasons.

Covered requirements:

* An author can adopt different names to publish books.
* An author can change their name due to marriage or other reasons.
* A book can have a title and an author.
* An author can have a pseudonym.
* A name change can occur on a specific date.

Known assumptions and exclusions:

* The ontology assumes that an author's name change is a one-time event.
* The ontology does not cover the specific reasons for a name change or the process of legally changing a name.
* The ontology does not include any information about the content or genre of the books.
* The ontology does not address the relationship between an author and their publisher or any other parties involved in the book publication process.
