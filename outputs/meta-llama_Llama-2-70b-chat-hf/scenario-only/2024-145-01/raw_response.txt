```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#>.
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#>.

@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#>.
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#>.

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
rdfs:label "Legal Task Ontology" ;
rdfs:comment "An ontology for modeling legal tasks and their related court hierarchies and bad check crime regulations.".

; Classes

:Court a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Court" ;
rdfs:comment "A court of law.".

rdfs:label "Bad Check Crime" ;

rdfs:label "Legal Task" ;
rdfs:comment "A task related to a legal case.".

; Properties

:hasCourt a owl:ObjectProperty ;
rdfs:label "has court" ;
rdfs:comment "A legal task has a court associated with it." ;
domain :LegalTask ;
range :Court.

:hasBadCheckCrime a owl:ObjectProperty ;
rdfs:label "has bad check crime" ;
rdfs:comment "A legal task has a bad check crime associated with it." ;
domain :LegalTask ;
range :BadCheckCrime.

:isRelatedTo a owl:ObjectProperty ;
rdfs:label "is related to" ;
rdfs:comment "A legal task is related to a bad check crime." ;
domain :LegalTask ;
range :BadCheckCrime.

:Court1 a :Court ;
rdfs:label "Court 1".

```
Documentation:

Intent: The Legal Task Ontology models the relationships between legal tasks, courts, and bad check crimes in a hierarchical manner, allowing for efficient querying and reasoning.

Covered Requirements:

* Legal tasks require managing complex information that is primarily unstructured in nature.
* Captures the court hierarchies and bad check crime related regulations.

Known Assumptions and Exclusions:

* The ontology assumes that there is a single court hierarchy.
* The ontology does not include any specific regulations or laws related to bad check crimes.
* The ontology does not include any other types of crimes or legal tasks beyond bad check crimes.
