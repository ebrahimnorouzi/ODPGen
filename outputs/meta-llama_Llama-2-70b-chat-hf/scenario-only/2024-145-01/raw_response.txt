Documentation:

Intent: The Legal Tasks Ontology models the complex information associated with legal tasks, including court hierarchies and bad check crime related regulations.

Covered requirements:

* Legal tasks require managing complex information that is primarily unstructured in nature.
* Captures the court hierarchies and bad check crime related regulations.

Known assumptions and exclusions:

* The ontology assumes that legal tasks are associated with a court and a court hierarchy.
* The ontology assumes that bad check crime is related to regulations.
* The ontology does not include instances of legal tasks or individuals involved in the legal tasks.
