Documentation:

Intent: The Cultural Heritage Survey ODP models the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities, including the relationships between surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes.

Covered requirements:

* Model the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities.
* Represent the relationships between surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes.

Known assumptions and exclusions:

* The ontology assumes that all surveys are performed on cultural heritage entities.
* The ontology does not include concepts related to the specific domain of cultural heritage, such as artifacts, monuments, or historical sites.
* The ontology does not include concepts related to the interpretation of data collected during a survey, such as analysis or conclusion drawing.
