Documentation:

Intent:
The Temporal Indirection Ontology models the relationship between persistent identifiers and the dynamic entities they refer to, accommodating various domains where temporal indirection is essential.

Covered Requirements:

* Persistent identifiers (CL1) serving as stable referents for dynamic entities (front-month crude oil futures contracts)
* Real-time market attributes (last trade price, trading volume, settlement value) bound to the current contract instance but exposed through the invariant identifier
* Cross-domain examples: healthcare, environmental, digital payment services, regulatory, air transportation
