Here is an OWL ontology design pattern (ODP) in Turtle syntax that supports the given competency questions:
```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#>.
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#>.
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>.
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#>.
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#>.

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
rdfs:label "Cultural Property Survey Ontology" ;
rdfs:comment "An ontology for representing data related to cultural property surveys.".

:Survey a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Survey" ;
rdfs:comment "A survey or investigation activity conducted on a cultural property or feature of interest.".

:Investigation a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Investigation" ;
rdfs:comment "A specific investigation technique or method adopted in a survey.".

:Observation a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Observation" ;
rdfs:comment "An observation produced during a survey or investigation activity.".

:Expert a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Expert" ;
rdfs:comment "An expert participant in a survey or investigation activity.".

:Hardware a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Hardware" ;
rdfs:comment "A hardware device used to conduct a survey or investigation activity.".

:Software a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Software" ;
rdfs:comment "A software tool used to conduct a survey or investigation activity.".

:TechnicalConfiguration a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Technical Configuration" ;
rdfs:comment "A specific configuration of hardware, software, and other technical components used in a survey or investigation activity.".

:PostProcessedOutput a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Post-Processed Output" ;
rdfs:comment "An output produced after processing data collected during a survey or investigation activity.".

:Acquisition a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Acquisition" ;
rdfs:comment "A specific acquisition cycle within a survey.".

:AcquisitionParameter a owl:Class ;
rdfs:label "Acquisition Parameter" ;
rdfs:comment "A parameter characterizing a specific acquisition cycle within a survey.".

:Survey -:hasInvestigation Investigation.
:Investigation -:hasObservation Observation.
:Observation -:hasExpert Expert.
:Survey -:hasHardware Hardware.
:Survey -:hasSoftware Software.
:Survey -:hasTechnicalConfiguration TechnicalConfiguration.
:PostProcessedOutput -:isDerivedFrom Observation.
:Acquisition -:hasAcquisitionParameter AcquisitionParameter.

* :PostProcessedOutput
```
