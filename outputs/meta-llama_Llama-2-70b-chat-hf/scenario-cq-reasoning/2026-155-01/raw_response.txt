Here's the OWL ontology design pattern (ODP) for the Cultural Heritage Survey scenario:

Reasoning steps:

1. Extract atomic requirements from the scenario:
* Survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities
* Relationships between surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes
2. Propose candidate classes and properties for each requirement:
* Survey:
	+ rdfs:label "Survey"
	+ rdfs:comment "A scientific survey activity performed on cultural heritage entities"
* Observation:
	+ rdfs:label "Observation"
	+ rdfs:comment "A piece of data produced during a survey activity"
* Method:
	+ rdfs:label "Method"
	+ rdfs:comment "An investigation technique adopted in a survey activity"
* EquipmentConfiguration:
	+ rdfs:label "EquipmentConfiguration"
	+ rdfs:comment "A specific set of hardware, software, and technical configurations used to conduct a survey activity"
* Expert:
	+ rdfs:label "Expert"
	+ rdfs:comment "An individual with a specific role in a survey activity"
* Interpretation:
	+ rdfs:label "Interpretation"
	+ rdfs:comment "An inference or conclusion drawn from observations produced during a survey activity"
3. Draft axioms for each candidate class and property:
* Survey:
	+ owl:Class rdfs:subClassOf owl:Survey.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Survey rdfs:range Observation.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Survey rdfs:range Method.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Survey rdfs:range EquipmentConfiguration.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Survey rdfs:range Expert.
* Observation:
	+ owl:Class rdfs:subClassOf owl:Observation.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Observation rdfs:range Data.
* Method:
	+ owl:Class rdfs:subClassOf owl:Method.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Method rdfs:range Technique.
* EquipmentConfiguration:
	+ owl:Class rdfs:subClassOf owl:EquipmentConfiguration.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain EquipmentConfiguration rdfs:range Hardware.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain EquipmentConfiguration rdfs:range Software.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain EquipmentConfiguration rdfs:range TechnicalConfiguration.
* Expert:
	+ owl:Class rdfs:subClassOf owl:Expert.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Expert rdfs:range Role.
* Interpretation:
	+ owl:Class rdfs:subClassOf owl:Interpretation.
	+ owl:ObjectProperty rdfs:domain Interpretation rdfs:range Observation.
