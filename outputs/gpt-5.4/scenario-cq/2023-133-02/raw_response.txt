1. ```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "kHealth Asthma causal monitoring ODP" ;
  rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern for representing asthma self-monitoring data about symptoms, medication intake, outdoor environmental allergens, and weighted causal relations among them." .

:CausalFactor a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Causal factor" ;
  rdfs:comment "A factor that can participate in weighted causal relations affecting another factor in the asthma monitoring setting." .

:Symptom a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Symptom" ;
  rdfs:comment "An asthma symptom observed or experienced by the patient, such as cough, chest tightness, wheezing, or breathing pattern." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor .

:MedicationIntake a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Medication intake" ;
  rdfs:comment "An intake of medication by the patient, represented as a factor that can be causally affected by symptoms or environmental allergens." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor .

:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Outdoor environmental allergen" ;
  rdfs:comment "An outdoor environmental feature relevant to asthma, such as pollen or air quality index, that can causally affect symptoms or medication intake." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor .

:CausalRelation a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Causal relation" ;
  rdfs:comment "A weighted causal relation linking a cause factor to an effect factor." .

:hasCause a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has cause" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a causal relation to the factor that acts as its cause." ;
  rdfs:domain :CausalRelation ;
  rdfs:range :CausalFactor .

:hasEffect a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has effect" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a causal relation to the factor that acts as its effect." ;
  rdfs:domain :CausalRelation ;
  rdfs:range :CausalFactor .

:causes a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "causes" ;
  rdfs:comment "Directly relates a causal factor to another causal factor that it causally affects." ;
  rdfs:domain :CausalFactor ;
  rdfs:range :CausalFactor .

:hasEffectWeight a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has effect weight" ;
  rdfs:comment "Associates a causal relation with a numeric weight expressing the strength of the causal effect." ;
  rdfs:domain :CausalRelation ;
  rdfs:range xsd:decimal .

:factorName a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
  rdfs:label "factor name" ;
  rdfs:comment "Provides a name or designation for a causal factor, such as cough, pollen, or AQI." ;
  rdfs:domain :CausalFactor ;
  rdfs:range xsd:string .

:hasCause owl:propertyChainAxiom ( :hasCause ) .

:hasEffect owl:propertyChainAxiom ( :hasEffect ) .

:causes owl:propertyChainAxiom ( :hasCause :hasEffect ) .
```

2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping:
- Requirement: Asthma monitoring involves multiple factors affecting a patient's condition.
  - Axioms:
    - `:CausalFactor a owl:Class`
    - `:Symptom rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor`
    - `:MedicationIntake rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor`
    - `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor`
- Requirement: Data collected include symptoms.
  - Axioms:
    - `:Symptom a owl:Class`
    - `:factorName a owl:DatatypeProperty ; rdfs:domain :CausalFactor`
- Requirement: Example symptoms include cough, chest tightness, wheezing, and breathing patterns.
  - Axioms:
    - `:Symptom rdfs:comment "... such as cough, chest tightness, wheezing, or breathing pattern."`
    - `:factorName` supports naming such symptom factors without creating individuals.
- Requirement: Data collected include intake of medications.
  - Axioms:
    - `:MedicationIntake a owl:Class`
- Requirement: Data collected include outdoor environment features such as pollen and AQI.
  - Axioms:
    - `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen a owl:Class`
    - `:factorName a owl:DatatypeProperty`
- Requirement: Edges represent causal relations.
  - Axioms:
    - `:CausalRelation a owl:Class`
    - `:hasCause a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:hasEffect a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:causes a owl:ObjectProperty`
- Requirement: Causal relations have effect weights.
  - Axioms:
    - `:hasEffectWeight a owl:DatatypeProperty ; rdfs:domain :CausalRelation ; rdfs:range xsd:decimal`
- Requirement: Example: pollen/AQI causes cough with weights 0.8 and 0.4.
  - Axioms:
    - `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor`
    - `:Symptom rdfs:subClassOf :CausalFactor`
    - `:CausalRelation`
    - `:hasCause`
    - `:hasEffect`
    - `:hasEffectWeight`
    - `:causes owl:propertyChainAxiom ( :hasCause :hasEffect )`

4. Intentionally excluded concepts:
- `Patient` / `Pediatric asthma patient`
  - Excluded because the scenario and CQs focus on causal relations among symptoms, medication intake, and outdoor allergens, not on modeling patient identity or demographics. Adding a patient class would go beyond what is needed to answer the CQs structurally.
- `Asthma` as a disease class
  - Excluded because the scenario provides domain context, but the CQs require modeling causal factors and their relations rather than a disease taxonomy.
- `Continuous self-monitoring project` / `kHealth-Asthma project` as organizational entities
  - Excluded because they are contextual background and not needed for the causal-question structure.
- `Presence` and `absence` as separate classes
  - Excluded because the scenario mentions presence of allergens and symptoms, but the CQs can be structurally supported through causal factors and weighted relations. Explicit counterfactual absence modeling would introduce additional constructs not explicitly grounded in the scenario.
- `Outdoor environment` as a separate class
  - Excluded because the scenario only requires representing outdoor environmental allergens/features as causal factors, not the environment as an independent entity.
- Separate classes for `Pollen` and `AQI`, or for `Cough`, `ChestTightness`, `Wheezing`, `BreathingPattern`
  - Excluded to avoid introducing domain subclasses or individuals not strictly required. The scenario and CQs only require that such factors be representable, which is supported via `:factorName` on the relevant factor classes.
