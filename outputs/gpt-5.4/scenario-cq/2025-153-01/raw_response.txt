2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping

- Requirement: A persistent identifier is a stable referent while the actual referenced entity changes over time.
  - Axioms:
    - `:PersistentIdentifier a owl:Class`
    - `:ReferentEntity a owl:Class`
    - `:TemporalBinding a owl:Class`
    - `:hasBinding a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:bindsIdentifier a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:bindsReferent a owl:ObjectProperty`

- Requirement: The identifier resolves to different referents during different time intervals.
  - Axioms:
    - `:TemporalBinding a owl:Class`
    - `:hasStartTime a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:hasEndTime a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:TimePoint a owl:Class`
    - `:timeValue a owl:DatatypeProperty`

- Requirement: Example of rollover from one futures contract to the next when expiration approaches.
  - Axioms:
    - `:TransitionEvent a owl:Class`
    - `:fromReferent a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:toReferent a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:occursAt a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:TransitionRule a owl:Class`
    - `:isTriggeredByRule a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:ruleDescription a owl:DatatypeProperty`

- Requirement: Real-time market attributes are bound to the current contract instance but exposed through the invariant identifier.
  - Axioms:
    - `:Observation a owl:Class`
    - `:hasObservation a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:observedReferent a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:observationTime a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:observationType a owl:DatatypeProperty`
    - `:observationValue a owl:DatatypeProperty`
    - Together with identifier-to-binding-to-referent path:
      `:hasBinding`, `:bindsReferent`, `:hasStartTime`, `:hasEndTime`

- Requirement: The pattern should generalize across domains such as healthcare, environmental sensing, digital payment services, regulation, and air transportation.
  - Axioms:
    - Use of domain-neutral classes:
      `:PersistentIdentifier`, `:ReferentEntity`, `:TemporalBinding`, `:TransitionEvent`, `:TransitionRule`, `:Observation`, `:TimePoint`

- Requirement: Continuity across successive changes must be representable as a sequence.
  - Axioms:
    - `:precedesBinding a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:hasTransition a owl:ObjectProperty`
    - `:transitionOfIdentifier a owl:ObjectProperty`

- Requirement: Complete context should be reconstructable for any time point.
  - Axioms:
    - `:TemporalBinding`, `:hasStartTime`, `:hasEndTime`
    - `:TransitionEvent`, `:occursAt`
    - `:Observation`, `:observationTime`
    - `:bindsReferent`, `:hasObservation`

4. Intentionally excluded concepts

- Domain-specific subclasses such as `CrudeOilFuturesContract`, `Physician`, `Sensor`, `APIVersion`, `Ruling`, `Aircraft`, or `Crew`
  - Reason: The scenario presents these as examples of the same pattern across domains. The CQs only require a generic notion of referent, so `:ReferentEntity` is sufficient.

- Separate classes for “current” versus “historical” referents
  - Reason: Current and past reference are determined by temporal bindings and timestamps, not by distinct ontological categories.

- A dedicated interval class
  - Reason: The scenario and CQs can be supported with start and end time points on `:TemporalBinding` without introducing an additional interval entity.

- Detailed quantitative datatypes for prices, volumes, or settlement values
  - Reason: The scenario mentions these as examples of observations, but the CQs only require associating observations with referents. Generic `:observationType` and `:observationValue` are enough.

- Provenance agents or systems performing the transition
  - Reason: Not grounded in the scenario or competency questions.

- Explicit logical constraints declaring gaps/overlaps as inconsistencies
  - Reason: The CQ asks whether gaps or overlaps can be detected, not that the ontology must enforce closed-world temporal validation. The structure supports checking them externally.

- Malpractice attribution, regulatory compliance, debugging, monitoring, or client stability as separate modeled concepts
  - Reason: These are motivations for using persistent identifiers in examples, not required entities for the temporal indirection pattern or the listed CQs.
