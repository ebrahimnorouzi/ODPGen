1. **Reasoning for steps 1–6**

### Step 1. Atomic requirements
- The ontology must represent a patient affected by asthma-related factors.
- The ontology must represent asthma symptoms.
- The ontology must represent medication intake.
- The ontology must represent outdoor environmental factors.
- Outdoor environmental allergens/features such as pollen and AQI must be representable.
- The ontology must represent causal relations between factors.
- A causal relation must support a numeric effect weight.
- Outdoor environmental factors can cause symptoms.
- Symptoms can affect medication intake.
- Outdoor environmental factors may affect medication intake directly or indirectly.
- The model should support asking for probable causes of a symptom such as cough.
- The model should support counterfactual-style questions about absence of a factor or symptom, at least structurally through causal links and weights.

### Step 2. Candidate classes and properties
- Classes:
  - `:Patient`
  - `:HealthFactor`
  - `:Symptom`
  - `:MedicationIntake`
  - `:EnvironmentalFactor`
  - `:CausalRelation`
- Candidate subclasses/individual-supporting classes:
  - `:Allergen` was considered, but pollen and AQI are both environmental factors and AQI is not necessarily an allergen; keep only `:EnvironmentalFactor`.
- Object properties:
  - `:hasFactor` (patient to health factor)
  - `:hasCause`
  - `:hasEffect`
  - `:affectsPatient` (environmental factor to patient) was considered, but not needed if effects are modeled through causal relations.
- Datatype properties:
  - `:effectWeight`
- Named example factor classes for CQ alignment:
  - `:Cough`
  - `:Pollen`
  - `:AQI`
- `:MedicationIntake` is best modeled as a class of factor/event rather than a datatype/property, because it participates in causal relations.

### Step 3. Draft axioms
- `:Symptom rdfs:subClassOf :HealthFactor`
- `:MedicationIntake rdfs:subClassOf :HealthFactor`
- `:EnvironmentalFactor rdfs:subClassOf :HealthFactor`
- `:Cough rdfs:subClassOf :Symptom`
- `:Pollen rdfs:subClassOf :EnvironmentalFactor`
- `:AQI rdfs:subClassOf :EnvironmentalFactor`
- Disjointness:
  - `:Patient owl:disjointWith :HealthFactor`
  - `:Symptom owl:disjointWith :MedicationIntake`
  - `:Symptom owl:disjointWith :EnvironmentalFactor`
  - `:MedicationIntake owl:disjointWith :EnvironmentalFactor`
- `:hasFactor` domain `:Patient`, range `:HealthFactor`
- `:hasCause` domain `:CausalRelation`, range `:HealthFactor`
- `:hasEffect` domain `:CausalRelation`, range `:HealthFactor`
- `:effectWeight` domain `:CausalRelation`, range `xsd:decimal`
- Cardinality/minimal structure for causal relation:
  - `:CausalRelation rdfs:subClassOf [ owl:onProperty :hasCause ; owl:qualifiedCardinality "1"^^xsd:nonNegativeInteger ; owl:onClass :HealthFactor ]`
  - `:CausalRelation rdfs:subClassOf [ owl:onProperty :hasEffect ; owl:qualifiedCardinality "1"^^xsd:nonNegativeInteger ; owl:onClass :HealthFactor ]`
  - `:CausalRelation rdfs:subClassOf [ owl:onProperty :effectWeight ; owl:maxCardinality "1"^^xsd:nonNegativeInteger ]`

### Step 4. CQ coverage check
- **CQ1: Is medication intake caused by outdoor environmental allergen such as pollen or AQI?**
  - Enabled by `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`, `:EnvironmentalFactor`, `:MedicationIntake`, and subclasses `:Pollen`, `:AQI`.
- **CQ2: How much does the presence of an asthma symptom effect the intake of medication?**
  - Enabled by `:CausalRelation` from `:Symptom` to `:MedicationIntake` plus `:effectWeight`.
- **CQ3: How does an outdoor environmental allergen affect the patient?**
  - Partly enabled: environmental factor can cause symptoms or medication intake, but “affect the patient” is not directly tied to patient-specific observations.
- **CQ4: What is the probable cause of the cough experienced by the patient?**
  - Partly enabled: can query causes of `:Cough`, but not explicitly tied to a patient’s experienced factor.
