**Step 1 — Atomic requirements**
- The pattern must represent survey or investigation activities.
- A survey activity may be performed on a cultural property.
- A survey activity may be performed on a specific feature of interest.
- A survey activity uses a method or investigation technique.
- A survey activity produces observations.
- Experts participate in a survey activity.
- Expert participation in a survey activity is qualified by a role.
- A survey activity uses hardware.
- A survey activity uses software.
- A survey activity uses a technical configuration.
- Observations can support interpretations or claims.
- Post-processed outputs are related to underlying acquisition or observation activities.
- A photographic acquisition cycle is a kind of activity within a survey context.
- A photographic acquisition cycle is characterised by acquisition parameters.
- Acquisition parameters include values such as exposure time, ISO, and aperture.

**Step 3 — Draft axioms**
- Class hierarchy:
  - `:PhotographicAcquisitionCycle rdfs:subClassOf :AcquisitionActivity`
- Domains and ranges:
  - `:isAboutCulturalProperty` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:CulturalProperty`
  - `:isAboutFeature` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:FeatureOfInterest`
  - `:usedMethod` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:Method`
  - `:producedObservation` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:Observation`
  - `:hasParticipation` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:Participation`
  - `:participant` domain `:Participation`, range `:Expert`
  - `:hasRole` domain `:Participation`, range `:Role`
  - `:usedHardware` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:Hardware`
  - `:usedSoftware` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:Software`
  - `:usedTechnicalConfiguration` domain `:SurveyActivity`, range `:TechnicalConfiguration`
  - `:supportsInterpretation` domain `:Observation`, range `:Interpretation`
  - `:derivedFromActivity` domain `:PostProcessedOutput`, range `:AcquisitionActivity`
  - `:derivedFromObservation` domain `:PostProcessedOutput`, range `:Observation`
  - `:hasAcquisitionParameter` domain `:PhotographicAcquisitionCycle`, range `:AcquisitionParameter`
  - `:parameterName` domain `:AcquisitionParameter`, range `xsd:string`
  - `:parameterValue` domain `:AcquisitionParameter`, range `xsd:string`
- Cardinality/minimal constraints:
  - `:Participation rdfs:subClassOf` exactly 1 `:participant`
  - `:Participation rdfs:subClassOf` exactly 1 `:hasRole`
  - `:AcquisitionParameter rdfs:subClassOf` exactly 1 `:parameterName`
  - `:AcquisitionParameter rdfs:subClassOf` min 1 `:parameterValue`
- Disjointness:
  - `:CulturalProperty`, `:FeatureOfInterest`, `:Method`, `:Observation`, `:Expert`, `:Participation`, `:Role`, `:Hardware`, `:Software`, `:TechnicalConfiguration`, `:Interpretation`, `:PostProcessedOutput`, `:AcquisitionParameter` are pairwise not all needed; keep only selected disjointness where useful.
  - Useful minimal disjointness:
    - `:Expert` disjoint with `:Role`
    - `:Observation` disjoint with `:Interpretation`
    - `:Hardware` disjoint with `:Software`
    - `:Participation` disjoint with `:SurveyActivity`
