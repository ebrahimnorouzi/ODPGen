1. ```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "Court Hierarchy and Bad Check Regulation ODP" ;
  rdfs:comment "A minimal ontology design pattern for representing court hierarchies, Indian states and districts, and bad check related check payment information." .

:State a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "State" ;
  rdfs:comment "A state-level administrative region." .

:District a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "District" ;
  rdfs:comment "A district administrative region located within a state." .

:Court a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Court" ;
  rdfs:comment "A judicial body within a court hierarchy." .

:DistrictCourt a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "District Court" ;
  rdfs:comment "A court operating at the district level." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :Court ,
    [ a owl:Restriction ;
      owl:onProperty :locatedInState ;
      owl:someValuesFrom :State
    ] .

:CheckPayment a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Check Payment" ;
  rdfs:comment "A payment transaction performed using a check." .

:BadCheck a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Bad Check" ;
  rdfs:comment "A check payment that is considered invalid or dishonored." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :CheckPayment ,
    [ a owl:Restriction ;
      owl:onProperty :hasTransactionType ;
      owl:someValuesFrom :TransactionType
    ] ,
    [ a owl:Restriction ;
      owl:onProperty :hasTransactionStatus ;
      owl:someValuesFrom :TransactionStatus
    ] ,
    [ a owl:Restriction ;
      owl:onProperty :paymentDate ;
      owl:someValuesFrom xsd:dateTime
    ] .

:TransactionType a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Transaction Type" ;
  rdfs:comment "A category describing the type of a check transaction." .

:TransactionStatus a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Transaction Status" ;
  rdfs:comment "A category describing the status of a check payment transaction." .

:locatedInState a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "located in state" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates an entity such as a district or district court to the state in which it is located." ;
  rdfs:domain owl:Thing ;
  rdfs:range :State .

:hasDistrict a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has district" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a state to a district that is part of it." ;
  rdfs:domain :State ;
  rdfs:range :District ;
  owl:inverseOf :locatedInState .

:hasAuthorityOver a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has authority over" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a court to another court over which it has authority." ;
  rdfs:domain :Court ;
  rdfs:range :Court .

:hasTransactionType a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has transaction type" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a check payment to its transaction type." ;
  rdfs:domain :CheckPayment ;
  rdfs:range :TransactionType .

:hasTransactionStatus a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has transaction status" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a check payment to its transaction status." ;
  rdfs:domain :CheckPayment ;
  rdfs:range :TransactionStatus .

:paymentDate a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
  rdfs:label "payment date" ;
  rdfs:comment "The date and time at which a check payment occurs." ;
  rdfs:domain :CheckPayment ;
  rdfs:range xsd:dateTime .

:State owl:disjointWith :District, :Court, :CheckPayment, :TransactionType, :TransactionStatus .
:District owl:disjointWith :Court, :CheckPayment, :TransactionType, :TransactionStatus .
:Court owl:disjointWith :CheckPayment, :TransactionType, :TransactionStatus .
:CheckPayment owl:disjointWith :TransactionType, :TransactionStatus .
:TransactionType owl:disjointWith :TransactionStatus .
```

2. Reasoning summary:
- 1. Extracted requirements for court hierarchy, state/district geography, court authority, and bad-check payment descriptors needed by the CQs.
- 2. Proposed minimal classes for State, District, Court, DistrictCourt, CheckPayment, BadCheck, TransactionType, and TransactionStatus plus location/authority/payment properties.
- 3. Drafted subclass, domain/range, inverse, existential restrictions, and disjointness axioms to constrain the pattern without adding instances.
- 4. Checked each CQ against the draft and found the need for explicit state–district linkage, district-court state linkage, authority relation, and payment date/type/status properties.
- 5. Refined with `hasDistrict` inverse of `locatedInState` and existential restrictions on `BadCheck` and `DistrictCourt` to support all CQs.
- 6. Removed any unneeded constructs such as separate regulation/document classes because they were not required by the scenario or CQs.
