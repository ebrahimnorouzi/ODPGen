1. **Reasoning for steps 1–6**

### Step 1. Atomic requirements extracted from the scenario
- The ontology must represent ships in a machine-interpretable way.
- The ontology must support clear identification of ships.
- The ontology must represent ship properties, including physical characteristics.
- The ontology must represent ship dimensions.
- The ontology must represent draught together with its unit.
- The ontology must represent a ship’s typical location.
- The ontology must represent ship ownership and owners.
- The ontology must represent flag state / registration under a flag.
- The ontology must represent average speed over ground for ships and for route/day observations.
- The ontology must represent routes.
- The ontology must represent trajectories and trajectory parts.
- The ontology must distinguish trajectory types.
- The ontology must represent predicted routes for ships.
- The ontology must represent relative placement of onboard equipment, specifically GPS antenna position with respect to bow/stern/port/starboard.
- The ontology must support counting ships by type and flag.
- The ontology must represent ship types, including container ships.
- The ontology must represent propulsion systems of ships.
- The ontology must represent engines as components of propulsion systems.
- The ontology must represent combinations/interconnections of engines.
- The ontology must represent what powers engines, i.e., fuels/energy carriers.
- The ontology must represent what is driven by engines.
- The ontology must distinguish propulsion systems that do not rely on an engine.
- The ontology must represent propulsion technologies used by propulsion systems.
- The ontology must distinguish propulsion technologies powered by engines.
- The ontology must represent jet propulsors and bladed propulsors.
- The ontology must represent special-design blade propulsors.
- The ontology must represent properties of bladed propulsors, including diameter and blade count.
- The ontology must represent screw propellers and fixed pitch propellers.
- The ontology must represent anti-cavitation devices/technologies for motorized propulsion technologies.
- The ontology must represent sails and sail types.
- The ontology must represent wind-assisted propulsion technologies.
- The ontology must support identifying ships with sails and ships using wind assistance.
- The ontology must support identifying ships using electric engines exclusively.
- The ontology must support identifying the motorized propulsion of a ship that is assisted by wind propulsion.
- The ontology must support counting engines powering a ship.
- The ontology must support identifying propeller types installed on a ship.
- The ontology must represent heterogeneous data resources about ships.
- The ontology must distinguish data kinds such as image, sonar, and textual information.
- The ontology must represent how sonar data is stored.
- The ontology must support linking ships to image resources.
- The ontology must support linking ships to textual descriptions.
- The ontology must support identifying ships without sonar signatures.
