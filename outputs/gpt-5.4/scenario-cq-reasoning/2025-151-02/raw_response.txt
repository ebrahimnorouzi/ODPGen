2. Reasoning summary:
- 1. Extracted requirements for policy evaluation context covering time, location, collections, actions, rule exercise, events, recipients, payments, and the scenario’s segmented video access for academic research.
- 2. Proposed minimal reusable classes for policy/rule/party/asset/event/payment/time plus collection and purpose constructs, with properties aligned to each CQ.
- 3. Drafted schema axioms using domains, ranges, subclassing, a few cardinalities, and selective disjointness to keep the pattern constrained but lightweight.
- 4. Checked each CQ against the model and identified missing support for current time, recipient categories, payment status/amount, and segmented asset use.
- 5. Added temporal context, recipient category, payment datatypes, and segmented asset interval/purpose axioms to ensure all CQs are answerable at schema level.
- 6. Removed non-traceable constructs and avoided individuals or overly specific ODRL commitments beyond what the scenario and CQs require.
