1. **Reasoning for steps 1–6**

- **Step 1: Atomic requirements**
  - R1. The model must represent a registry that can be maintained by some actor.
  - R2. The model must represent creation and update timestamps for a registry.
  - R3. The model must represent types/categories of data available.
  - R4. The model must represent where a specific data type is stored.
  - R5. The model must represent a policy associated with data.
  - R6. The model should stay minimal and reusable, avoiding unnecessary domain-specific commitments.

- **Step 2: Candidate classes and properties**
  - For R1:
    - Classes: `:Registry`, `:Actor`
    - Property: `:maintainedBy`
  - For R2:
    - Class: `:Registry`
    - Datatype properties: `:createdAt`, `:updatedAt`
  - For R3:
    - Classes: `:DataCategory`
    - Property: `:hasAvailableDataCategory`
  - For R4:
    - Classes: `:StorageLocation`
    - Property: `:storedAt`
  - For R5:
    - Class: `:Policy`
    - Property: `:hasPolicy`
  - For connecting data category to policy and storage in a minimal way:
    - Treat `:DataCategory` as the thing whose storage location and policy are described.

- **Step 3: Draft axioms**
  - `:Registry` and `:Actor`, `:DataCategory`, `:StorageLocation`, `:Policy` as owl classes.
  - `:maintainedBy` with domain `:Registry` and range `:Actor`.
  - `:createdAt` with domain `:Registry` and range `xsd:dateTime`.
  - `:updatedAt` with domain `:Registry` and range `xsd:dateTime`.
  - `:hasAvailableDataCategory` with domain `:Registry` and range `:DataCategory`.
  - `:storedAt` with domain `:DataCategory` and range `:StorageLocation`.
  - `:hasPolicy` with domain `:DataCategory` and range `:Policy`.
  - Cardinality:
    - A registry may have at most one creation timestamp.
    - A registry may have at most one update timestamp.
  - Minimal disjointness:
    - `:Actor`, `:Registry`, `:DataCategory`, `:StorageLocation`, `:Policy` pairwise disjoint enough to avoid category confusion.

- **Step 4: CQ coverage check**
  - CQ1. *Who is maintaining the registry?*
    - Enabled by `:maintainedBy` domain/range.
  - CQ2. *When was the registry created/updated?*
    - Enabled by `:createdAt`, `:updatedAt`.
  - CQ3. *What types of data are available?*
    - Enabled by `:hasAvailableDataCategory`.
  - CQ4. *Where is a specific type of data being stored?*
    - Enabled by `:storedAt` from `:DataCategory` to `:StorageLocation`.
  - CQ5. *What policy is associated with the data?*
    - Enabled by `:hasPolicy` from `:DataCategory` to `:Policy`.
  - No CQ remains uncovered.

- **Step 5: Add/refine axioms**
  - Add existential restrictions on `:Registry` to indicate that a registry is intended to describe available data categories and may identify a maintainer.
  - Keep these weak/minimal:
    - `:Registry rdfs:subClassOf [ owl:onProperty :hasAvailableDataCategory ; owl:someValuesFrom :DataCategory ]`
  - Do not force every registry to have maintainer or timestamps, since scenario/CQs ask for representation, not mandatory completeness.

- **Step 6: Remove untraceable elements**
  - Removed any notions about apps, pods, consent logs, legal compliance, specifications, and functionality installation because they are not needed for the given CQs.
  - Kept only: `:Registry`, `:Actor`, `:DataCategory`, `:StorageLocation`, `:Policy`, and the five core properties plus timestamps.
