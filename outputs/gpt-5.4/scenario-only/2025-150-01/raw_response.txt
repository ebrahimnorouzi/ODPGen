```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .

@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "MQTT Process Data ODP" ;
  rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern for modeling automated process data captured and transmitted via MQTT packets, including alignment with observation and actuation process descriptions and payload format and encoding characteristics." .

:ProcessDescription a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Process Description" ;
  rdfs:comment "A description of an automated process relevant to captured and transmitted data." .

:ObservationProcessDescription a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Observation Process Description" ;
  rdfs:comment "A process description concerned with observation." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :ProcessDescription .

:ActuationProcessDescription a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Actuation Process Description" ;
  rdfs:comment "A process description concerned with actuation." ;
  rdfs:subClassOf :ProcessDescription .

:MQTTPacket a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "MQTT Packet" ;
  rdfs:comment "A packet used to capture and transmit automated process data via MQTT." .

:Payload a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Payload" ;

:ObservationProcessDescription owl:disjointWith :ActuationProcessDescription .
:PlainTextFormat owl:disjointWith :CSVFormat .
:PlainTextFormat owl:disjointWith :JSONFormat .
:CSVFormat owl:disjointWith :JSONFormat .
```

**Intent:** Model automated process data transmitted via MQTT packets, including alignment to observation and actuation process descriptions, transport semantics, and payload format and encoding.

**Covered requirements:**
- Models MQTT as a publish-subscribe transport context through `MQTTPacket` and `TransportSemantics`.
- Supports alignment between observation and actuation process descriptions and transport semantics.
- Represents automated process data captured and transmitted via MQTT packets.
- Models MQTT payloads separately from packets.
- Captures payload format variation including plain text, CSV, and structured JSON.
- Captures payload character set encoding as a separate concern.
- Stays scoped to the EKI project context of automated process data modeling.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- MQTT topics, brokers, publishers, subscribers, QoS levels, and packet subtypes are excluded because they are not explicitly required by the scenario.
- Payload formats are modeled as classes, not enumerated individuals, to satisfy the no-instances requirement.
- Character set encodings are represented generically without naming specific encodings such as UTF-8.
- Observation and actuation are modeled as process descriptions rather than full execution events or workflows.
- No cardinality restrictions were added, as the scenario does not justify stricter constraints.
