2. **Documentation**

**Intent:**  
This pattern models ships, their properties, trajectories, data sources, and propulsion system structures as a common semantic backbone for integrating heterogeneous maritime data.

**Covered requirements:**
- Represent ships in a formalized, machine-interpretable conceptual model.
- Support standardized descriptions of vessel characteristics for fleet management and operational planning.
- Represent vessel trajectory data for semantic querying in search and rescue scenarios.
- Provide a common semantic layer across heterogeneous maritime data sources such as AIS, registries, technical specifications, classification databases, and onboard monitoring platforms.
- Model propulsion systems and their interconnections to support digital twins and life cycle simulations.
- Improve interoperability, reuse, and automated reasoning through a unified semantic structure.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- The pattern stays at a generic schema level and does not model specific operational processes such as logistics workflows, SAR events, or research activities.
- Specific ship types, detailed technical parameters, and detailed trajectory geometry/time structures are not introduced because they are not explicitly required by the scenario.
- Data source categories are represented generically as `DataSource` rather than as separate subclasses for each named source type.
- Propulsion systems are modeled structurally and generically, without introducing finer-grained component taxonomies beyond interconnection.
