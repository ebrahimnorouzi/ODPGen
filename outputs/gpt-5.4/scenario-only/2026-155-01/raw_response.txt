```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
    rdfs:label "Cultural Heritage Survey ODP" ;
    rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern for modeling the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities, including surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes." .

:Survey a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Survey" ;
    rdfs:comment "A scientific survey activity performed on a cultural heritage entity." .

:CulturalHeritageEntity a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Cultural Heritage Entity" ;
    rdfs:comment "A cultural heritage entity that is the subject of a scientific survey." .

:Observation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "An observation produced or carried out within the context of a survey." .

:Method a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Method" ;
    rdfs:comment "A method used in a survey or observation." .

:EquipmentConfiguration a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Equipment Configuration" ;
    rdfs:comment "A configuration of equipment used in a survey or observation." .

:InterpretationProcess a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Interpretation Process" ;
    rdfs:comment "A process in which observations are interpreted in the context of a survey." .

:surveysEntity a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "surveys entity" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a survey to the cultural heritage entity it investigates." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :CulturalHeritageEntity .

:hasObservation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a survey to an observation carried out as part of it." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :Observation .

:usesMethod a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "uses method" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a survey, observation, or interpretation process to a method it uses." ;
    rdfs:range :Method .

:usesEquipmentConfiguration a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "uses equipment configuration" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a survey or observation to an equipment configuration used in it." ;
    rdfs:range :EquipmentConfiguration .

:hasInterpretationProcess a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has interpretation process" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a survey to an interpretation process associated with it." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :InterpretationProcess .

:interpretsObservation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "interprets observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates an interpretation process to an observation that it interprets." ;
    rdfs:domain :InterpretationProcess ;
    rdfs:range :Observation .

:Survey owl:disjointWith :CulturalHeritageEntity, :Observation, :Method, :EquipmentConfiguration, :InterpretationProcess .
:CulturalHeritageEntity owl:disjointWith :Observation, :Method, :EquipmentConfiguration, :InterpretationProcess .
:Observation owl:disjointWith :Method, :EquipmentConfiguration, :InterpretationProcess .
:Method owl:disjointWith :EquipmentConfiguration, :InterpretationProcess .
:EquipmentConfiguration owl:disjointWith :InterpretationProcess .

:usesMethod rdfs:domain [
    a owl:Class ;
    owl:unionOf ( :Survey :Observation :InterpretationProcess )
] .

:usesEquipmentConfiguration rdfs:domain [
    a owl:Class ;
    owl:unionOf ( :Survey :Observation )
] .
```

**Intent:**  
Model the core structure of scientific survey activities on cultural heritage entities, including surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes.

**Covered requirements:**
- Models scientific survey activities.
- Represents cultural heritage entities as subjects of surveys.
- Represents observations related to surveys.
- Represents methods used in survey-related activities.
- Represents equipment configurations used in surveys or observations.
- Represents interpretation processes connected to surveys and observations.
- Captures relationships among surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- The pattern focuses only on structural relations explicitly mentioned in the scenario.
