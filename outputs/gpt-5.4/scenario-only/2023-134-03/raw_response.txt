**Intent:** Model governance-relevant entities and relations for Solid Pods and apps, focusing on actor identity, infrastructure metadata, data category discovery, privacy declarations, user privacy policies, provenance/accountability records, and compliance support.

**Covered requirements:**
- Represent Solid actors and their roles, including users and providers.
- Represent Pod providers and app providers for identifying who provides Pods and apps.
- Represent Pod metadata about Solid specifications and installed functionalities.
- Represent data categories and where they are stored in Pods.
- Represent machine-readable privacy notices and declared data handling practices.
- Represent app-declared data requirements needed to function.
- Represent user-expressed privacy policies, preferences, and requirements.
- Represent incoming data requests and decisions about them.
- Represent provenance/accountability records, including consent, access, and policy change records.
- Represent who accessed data and what they are doing with it.
- Represent GDPR-related rights such as consent withdrawal.
- Represent auditing information, compliance checks, and investigations by authorities.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- The pattern stays at schema level only and does not include individuals or operational workflows.
- “Role” is modeled generically because the scenario mentions roles but does not enumerate them.
- Privacy notices are attached generically via `:hasPrivacyNotice` without constraining the domain to a union of Pod and app, to keep axioms minimal.
- Legal compliance is modeled only at a high level; no detailed GDPR taxonomy or rule logic is introduced.
- No temporal, versioning, or provenance standards are imported, since they are not explicitly required by the scenario.
