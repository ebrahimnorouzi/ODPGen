**Intent:** Model governance-relevant concepts for Solid apps and Pods that address the seven identified challenges around actor identity, infrastructure metadata, data discovery, data handling transparency, user privacy expression, accountability logging, and legal compliance support.

**Covered requirements:**
- Represent Solid actors and their roles.
- Represent Pod providers and app providers.
- Represent Pods and Solid apps.
- Represent metadata about Pod infrastructure, including Solid specification and installed functionalities.
- Represent categories of data and where they are stored in a Pod.
- Represent machine-readable privacy notices and data handling practices.
- Represent declarations of what data an app needs to function.
- Represent user-expressed privacy policies and privacy preferences.
- Represent incoming data requests and decisions about data use.
- Represent provenance and accountability records, including consent, access, data use, and policy changes.
- Represent compliance checks, GDPR-related rights such as consent withdrawal, and audit information for authorities.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- The pattern stays at schema level only and does not include individuals.
- “Who accessed data” and “what they are doing with it” are modeled minimally through access and data use records linked to actors.
- The scenario mentions Pods and apps both lacking some governance information; where a single property may apply to both, the pattern avoids artificial superclasses and leaves domain unspecified.
- No detailed GDPR taxonomy, consent workflow, provenance vocabulary alignment, or access-control logic is added, because these are not explicitly required by the scenario.
- No temporal, versioning, or event sequencing model is included beyond the existence of records and checks.
