Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping:
1. Identity of Solid actors and their roles is unknown – :SolidActor, :Role
2. No metadata about Solid infrastructure – :Registry
3. Availability/Discovery of categories of data – :DataCategory, :dataCategory
4. Pod and app providers do not provide information on their data handling practices – :DataHandlingPolicy, :associatedPolicy

CQ-to-axiom mapping:
- Who is maintaining the registry? :maintainer
- When was the registry created/updated? :creationDate
- What types of data are available? :DataCategory
- Where is a specific type of data being stored? :storageLocation
- What policy is associated with the data? :associatedPolicy

Intentionally excluded concepts:
- Individual Solid actors, Pods, and data instances as the scenario does not require the creation of individual instances.
- Specific data handling practices as they are not required to be modeled in the ontology.
