**Turtle Ontology Design Pattern (ODP) - Cultural Heritage Survey**

```turtle
:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "Cultural Heritage Survey ODP" ;
  rdfs:comment "Models the structure of scientific survey activities on cultural heritage entities.".

:Survey a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Survey" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a scientific survey activity on cultural heritage." ;
  owl:disjointWith :EquipmentConfiguration.

:Observation a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Observation" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents an observation made during a survey." ;
  owl:disjointWith :Survey ;
  owl:disjointWith :Method ;
  owl:disjointWith :InterpretationProcess.

:Method a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Method" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a scientific method used in a survey." ;
  owl:disjointWith :Survey ;
  owl:disjointWith :Observation ;
  owl:disjointWith :EquipmentConfiguration ;
  owl:disjointWith :InterpretationProcess.

:EquipmentConfiguration a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Equipment Configuration" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents the configuration of equipment used in a survey." ;
  owl:disjointWith :Survey ;
  owl:disjointWith :Observation ;
  owl:disjointWith :Method ;
  owl:disjointWith :InterpretationProcess.

:InterpretationProcess a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Interpretation Process" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents the process of interpreting survey results." ;
  owl:disjointWith :Survey ;
  owl:disjointWith :Observation ;
  owl:disjointWith :Method ;
  owl:disjointWith :EquipmentConfiguration.

:hasSurvey a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "has Survey" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates an observation, method, equipment configuration, or interpretation process to the survey it belongs to." ;
  owl:domain :Observation ;
  owl:domain :Method ;
  owl:domain :EquipmentConfiguration ;
  owl:domain :InterpretationProcess ;
  owl:inverseOf :performedBySurvey.

:performedBySurvey a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:label "performed by Survey" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relates a survey to the observations, methods, equipment configurations, or interpretation processes it performed." ;
  owl:domain :Survey ;
  owl:range :Observation ;
  owl:range :Method ;
  owl:range :EquipmentConfiguration ;
  owl:range :InterpretationProcess.
```

**Documentation**

- Intent: The Cultural Heritage Survey ODP models the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities, including relationships between surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes.
- Covered requirements:
  - Represents a scientific survey activity on cultural heritage (`:Survey` class).
  - Represents an observation made during a survey (`:Observation` class).
  - Represents a scientific method used in a survey (`:Method` class).
  - Represents the configuration of equipment used in a survey (`:EquipmentConfiguration` class).
  - Represents the process of interpreting survey results (`:InterpretationProcess` class).
  - Relates an observation, method, equipment configuration, or interpretation process to the survey it belongs to (`:hasSurvey` property).
  - Relates a survey to the observations, methods, equipment configurations, or interpretation processes it performed (`:performedBySurvey` property).
- Known assumptions and exclusions: This ODP assumes that each survey, observation, method, equipment configuration, and interpretation process is unique within the ontology. It does not include concepts for cultural heritage entities themselves, as they are not explicitly mentioned in the scenario.
