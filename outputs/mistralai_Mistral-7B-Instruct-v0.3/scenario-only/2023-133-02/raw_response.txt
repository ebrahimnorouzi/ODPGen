**Turtle Ontology Design Pattern (ODP)**

```turtle
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#>.
@prefix rdf: <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#>.
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>.
@prefix xsd: <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#>.
@prefix : <http://example.org/odp#>.

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "kHealth-Asthma ODP" ;
  rdfs:comment "An ontology for modeling asthma patients, their symptoms, medication intake, and outdoor environmental factors in the kHealth-Asthma project.".

:AsthmaPatient a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Asthma Patient" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a pediatric asthma patient.".

:Symptom a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Symptom" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a symptom experienced by an asthma patient.".

:Medication a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Medication" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a medication taken by an asthma patient.".

:OutdoorEnvironmentFactor a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Outdoor Environment Factor" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents an outdoor environmental factor affecting an asthma patient.".

:hasSymptom a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:domain :AsthmaPatient ;
  rdfs:range :Symptom ;
  rdfs:label "Has Symptom" ;
  rdfs:comment "Indicates that an asthma patient has a specific symptom.".

:takesMedication a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:domain :AsthmaPatient ;
  rdfs:range :Medication ;
  rdfs:label "Takes Medication" ;
  rdfs:comment "Indicates that an asthma patient takes a specific medication.".

:isOutdoorEnvironmentFactor a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:domain :AsthmaPatient ;
  rdfs:range :OutdoorEnvironmentFactor ;
  rdfs:label "Is Outdoor Environment Factor" ;
  rdfs:comment "Indicates that an outdoor environmental factor affects an asthma patient.".

:hasCausalEffectWeight a owl:ObjectProperty ;
  rdfs:domain [ a :OutdoorEnvironmentFactor ; owl:oneOf (:Pollen ; :AQI) ] ;
  rdfs:range xsd:decimal ;
  rdfs:label "Has Causal Effect Weight" ;
  rdfs:comment "Indicates the causal effect weight of an outdoor environmental factor on a symptom.".

:Pollen a :OutdoorEnvironmentFactor ;
  rdfs:label "Pollen" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents pollen in the outdoor environment.".

:AQI a :OutdoorEnvironmentFactor ;
  rdfs:label "Air Quality Index (AQI)" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents the air quality index in the outdoor environment.".
```

**Documentation**

- Intent: This pattern models asthma patients, their symptoms, medication intake, and outdoor environmental factors in the kHealth-Asthma project, with causal relations and effect weights between outdoor environmental factors and symptoms.
- Covered requirements:
  - Models asthma patients, symptoms, medications, and outdoor environmental factors.
  - Defines properties for relating asthma patients to their symptoms, medications, and outdoor environmental factors.
  - Defines properties for representing causal relations and effect weights between outdoor environmental factors and symptoms.
- Known assumptions and exclusions:
  - This pattern assumes that the ontology is used within the kHealth-Asthma project context and does not account for other potential factors affecting
