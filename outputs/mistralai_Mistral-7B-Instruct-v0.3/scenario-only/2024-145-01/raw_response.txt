**Turtle Ontology Design Pattern (ODP)**

```turtle
:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "Legal Tasks and Court Hierarchies" ;
  rdfs:comment "This ontology models the complex information required for legal tasks, focusing on court hierarchies and bad check crime regulations.".

:Court a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Court" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a court in the legal system.".

:AppellateCourt a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Appellate Court" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents an appellate court in the legal system." ;
  owl:disjointWith :Court.

:DistrictCourt a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "District Court" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a district court in the legal system." ;
  owl:disjointWith :Court.

:BadCheckRegulation a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Bad Check Regulation" ;
  rdfs:comment "Represents a regulation related to bad check crimes.".

:HasJurisdiction rdf:Property ;
  rdfs:label "Has Jurisdiction" ;
  rdfs:comment "Indicates the court that has jurisdiction over a specific case." ;
  owl:domain :Court ;
  owl:range :Court.

:ImplementsRegulation rdf:Property ;
  rdfs:label "Implements Regulation" ;
  rdfs:comment "Indicates a court that implements a specific regulation." ;
  owl:domain :Court ;
  owl:range :BadCheckRegulation.

```

**Documentation**

- Intent: This pattern models the court hierarchies and bad check crime regulations within a legal system.
- Covered requirements:
  - Models court hierarchies (Appellate Court and District Court).
  - Represents unstructured legal information (court jurisdictions and regulations).
- Known assumptions and exclusions:
  - This pattern assumes a simple court hierarchy with only appellate and district courts.
  - It does not model specific cases or legal proceedings.
  - It does not cover other types of legal information or court structures.
