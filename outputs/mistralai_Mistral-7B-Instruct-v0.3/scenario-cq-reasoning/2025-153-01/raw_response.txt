1. Atomic Requirements:
   - Persistent Identifiers (PID) have a unique string value.
   - PIDs refer to specific entities (Contract, Physician, Sensor, etc.).
   - Entities have a temporal scope (validity period).
   - PIDs can transition to refer to different entities as their temporal scope changes.
   - Observations are associated with specific entities.
   - The history of PID referents can be traced over time.
   - Rules govern PID transitions.
   - There may be gaps or overlaps in temporal bindings.
   - Complete context can be rebuilt for any time point.
