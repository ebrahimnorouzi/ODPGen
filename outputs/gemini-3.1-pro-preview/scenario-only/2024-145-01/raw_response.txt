```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
    rdfs:label "Legal Tasks and Bad Check Crime Regulations Ontology Pattern" ;
    rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern representing legal tasks, unstructured information, court hierarchies, and regulations related to bad check crimes." .

#################################################################
#    Classes
#################################################################

:LegalTask a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Legal Task" ;
    rdfs:comment "A task or activity performed within a legal context." .

:Information a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Information" ;
    rdfs:comment "Data or knowledge managed during legal tasks." .

:UnstructuredInformation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:subClassOf :Information ;
    rdfs:label "Unstructured Information" ;
    rdfs:comment "Complex information that does not have a pre-defined data model or is not organized in a pre-defined manner." .

:Court a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Court" ;
    rdfs:comment "A tribunal presided over by a judge, judges, or a magistrate in civil and criminal cases." .

:Regulation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Regulation" ;
    rdfs:comment "A rule or directive made and maintained by an authority." .

:Crime a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Crime" ;
    rdfs:comment "An action or omission that constitutes an offense that may be prosecuted by the state and is punishable by law." .

:BadCheckCrime a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:subClassOf :Crime ;
    rdfs:label "Bad Check Crime" ;
    rdfs:comment "A specific type of crime involving the intentional issuance of a check for which there are insufficient funds." .

#################################################################
#    Disjointness
#################################################################

[] a owl:AllDisjointClasses ;
    owl:members ( :LegalTask :Information :Court :Regulation :Crime ) .

#################################################################
#    Object Properties
#################################################################

:requiresInformation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :LegalTask ;
    rdfs:range :Information ;
    rdfs:label "requires information" ;
    rdfs:comment "Indicates the information that is necessary to perform a legal task." .

:capturesCourt a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Information ;
    rdfs:range :Court ;
    rdfs:label "captures court" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links information to the court entities it describes or captures." .

:capturesRegulation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Information ;
    rdfs:range :Regulation ;
    rdfs:label "captures regulation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links information to the legal regulations it describes or captures." .

:hasSubCourt a owl:ObjectProperty , owl:TransitiveProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Court ;
    rdfs:range :Court ;
    owl:inverseOf :isSubCourtOf ;
    rdfs:label "has sub-court" ;
    rdfs:comment "Establishes a hierarchical relationship where one court is structurally or jurisdictionally above another." .

:isSubCourtOf a owl:ObjectProperty , owl:TransitiveProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Court ;
    rdfs:range :Court ;
    rdfs:label "is sub-court of" ;
    rdfs:comment "Establishes a hierarchical relationship where one court is structurally or jurisdictionally below another." .

:regulatesCrime a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Regulation ;
    rdfs:range :Crime ;
    rdfs:label "regulates crime" ;
    rdfs:comment "Associates a regulation with the specific crime it governs or relates to." .
```

### Documentation

**Intent:**
To model the relationship between legal tasks and the unstructured information they require, specifically capturing hierarchical court structures and regulations governing bad check crimes.

**Covered requirements:**
*   Models `LegalTask` and its dependency on `Information` (specifically `UnstructuredInformation`).
*   Captures court hierarchies using transitive properties (`hasSubCourt` and `isSubCourtOf`).
*   Models regulations and their specific relationship to crimes, including the specialized `BadCheckCrime`.
*   Connects the unstructured information to the domain concepts (courts and regulations) it captures.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
*   **Assumption:** The pronoun "It" in the scenario ("It captures the court hierarchies...") is interpreted to mean the *Information* managed by the legal tasks, rather than the legal task itself.
*   **Assumption:** Court hierarchies are strictly modeled as transitive relationships between `Court` entities.
*   **Exclusion:** The pattern excludes specific jurisdictional boundaries, roles of legal personnel, or the specific penalties associated with bad check crimes, as these were not explicitly mentioned in the scenario.
*   **Exclusion:** Structured information is not explicitly modeled, as the scenario strictly emphasizes the primarily unstructured nature of the information.
