### Documentation

**Intent:** 
To model the continuous self-monitoring data of pediatric asthma patients, capturing their symptoms, medication intake, environmental exposures, and the weighted causal relationships between the environment and the patients' health conditions.

**Covered requirements:**
*   Representation of pediatric asthma patients.
*   Representation of specific asthma symptoms (cough, chest tightness, wheezing, breathing patterns).
*   Representation of medication intake events.
*   Representation of outdoor environment features (pollen, AQI).
*   Modeling of causal relations (edges) with specific effect weights (e.g., 0.8, 0.4) linking environmental features to symptoms or medication intake.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
*   **Assumption (N-ary Relation):** Because OWL does not natively support properties on properties (e.g., adding a weight directly to a `causes` object property), the causal edges are modeled using an n-ary relation pattern via the `CausalRelation` class. This allows the assignment of a specific `hasEffectWeight` to the relationship.
*   **Assumption (Effect Range):** The scenario states that environmental allergens affect "the patient's symptoms and their medication intake." Therefore, the range of the `hasEffect` property is modeled as a union of `Symptom` and `MedicationIntake`.
*   **Exclusion (Temporal Data):** Although the scenario mentions "continuous self-monitoring," specific temporal concepts (like timestamps or observation periods) were excluded to strictly adhere to the provided scope without inventing unmentioned classes.
*   **Exclusion (Instances):** As requested, no individual patients, specific weight instances, or specific symptom occurrences are instantiated; the output is strictly the schema/pattern.
