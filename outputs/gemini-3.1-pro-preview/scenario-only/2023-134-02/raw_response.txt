### Documentation

**Intent:** 
To model the actors, infrastructure, data categories, privacy policies, and provenance metadata required to address governance, transparency, and legal compliance challenges in Solid applications.

**Covered requirements:**
*   **C1:** Identification of Solid actors (`SolidUser`, `PodProvider`, `AppProvider`) and their relationships to infrastructure (`providesPod`, `providesApp`, `usesApp`).
*   **C2:** Representation of Solid infrastructure metadata, including specifications (`SolidSpecification`) and installed features (`PodFunctionality`).
*   **C3:** Discovery of data categories (`DataCategory`) within Pods and their storage locations (`dataLocation`).
*   **C4:** Declaration of data handling practices (`DataHandlingPractice`), required data for apps (`requiresDataCategory`), and machine-readable privacy notices (`PrivacyNotice`, `isMachineReadable`).
*   **C5:** Expression of user privacy policies (`PrivacyPolicy`), management of incoming data requests (`DataRequest`), and data use decisions (`DataUseDecision`).
*   **C6:** Logging of provenance metadata (`ProvenanceMetadata`) within Pods, including consent records, data access logs, and policy change logs.
*   **C7:** Facilitation of legal compliance checks by allowing users to exercise GDPR rights (`GDPRRight`) and providing authorities (`Authority`) with auditing information (`AuditingInformation`).

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
*   **Assumptions:** It is assumed that provenance metadata and logs are stored directly within the user's Solid Pod (`recordsProvenance` domain is `SolidPod`). It is also assumed that Pod Providers and App Providers can be distinctly categorized, though a single real-world entity could theoretically instantiate both classes.
*   **Exclusions:** The pattern excludes the granular modeling of specific GDPR rights (e.g., Right to Erasure vs. Right to Portability) or specific Solid specifications (e.g., WAC vs. ACP), focusing strictly on the high-level governance and compliance framework requested in the scenario. Individual instances are excluded by design.
