```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
    rdfs:label "Temporal Indirection Ontology Design Pattern" ;
    rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern for modeling persistent identifiers that dynamically resolve to different referents over time, tracking transitions, rules, and observations." .

# Classes

:PersistentIdentifier a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Persistent Identifier" ;
    rdfs:comment "A stable identifier that dynamically resolves to different entities over time (e.g., CL1, UA001)." .

:Referent a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Referent" ;
    rdfs:comment "The specific entity that a persistent identifier resolves to during a specific time period (e.g., Feb 2025 contract, specific aircraft)." .

:TemporalBinding a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Temporal Binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "An n-ary relationship class representing the time-bound assignment of a persistent identifier to a specific referent." .

:Observation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Real-time attributes, events, or measurements bound to a specific referent." .

:TransitionRule a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Transition Rule" ;
    rdfs:comment "The rule, event, or condition that triggered the transition to a new temporal binding." .

# Object Properties

:hasBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a persistent identifier to its temporal bindings." ;
    rdfs:domain :PersistentIdentifier ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding .

:resolvesTo a owl:ObjectProperty , owl:FunctionalProperty ;
    rdfs:label "resolves to" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a temporal binding to the specific referent it targets." ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :Referent .

:hasObservation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a referent to its specific observations or attributes." ;
    rdfs:domain :Referent ;
    rdfs:range :Observation .

:triggeredBy a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "triggered by" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a temporal binding to the rule or event that caused its creation." ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :TransitionRule .

:nextBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "next binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a temporal binding to the subsequent binding in the sequence." ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding ;
    owl:inverseOf :previousBinding .

:previousBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "previous binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a temporal binding to the preceding binding in the sequence." ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding .

# Datatype Properties

:validFrom a owl:DatatypeProperty , owl:FunctionalProperty ;
    rdfs:label "valid from" ;
    rdfs:comment "The start timestamp of the temporal binding." ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime .

:validTo a owl:DatatypeProperty , owl:FunctionalProperty ;
    rdfs:label "valid to" ;
    rdfs:comment "The end timestamp of the temporal binding. May be empty if currently active." ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime .

# Disjointness

[] a owl:AllDisjointClasses ;
    owl:members ( :PersistentIdentifier :Referent :TemporalBinding :Observation :TransitionRule ) .
```

### Reasoning Summary
1. **Extract atomic requirements:** Identified needs for stable identifiers, dynamic referent resolution, temporal tracking, transition triggers, and referent-bound observations.
2. **Propose candidate classes and properties:** Created `PersistentIdentifier`, `Referent`, `TemporalBinding` (n-ary relation), `Observation`, and `TransitionRule`, linked by properties like `hasBinding`, `resolvesTo`, and `validFrom`.
3. **Draft axioms:** Defined domains, ranges, functional characteristics (e.g., a binding resolves to exactly one referent), and global disjointness among core classes.
4. **Verify against CQs:** Checked that temporal queries can be answered via `validFrom`/`validTo`, sequences via `nextBinding`, and context via `hasObservation`.
5. **Add/refine axioms:** Added `nextBinding` and `previousBinding` as inverse object properties to explicitly support querying the full sequence of referents over time.
6. **Remove unused elements:** Ensured no extraneous classes (like specific domains e.g., Healthcare or Finance) were included, keeping the pattern strictly generic and minimal.
