### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping

*   **C1 (Identity of Solid actors and roles):** Encoded via the `:SolidActor` class and its subclasses (`:User`, `:PodProvider`, `:AppProvider`). The relationships between these actors and the infrastructure are captured by `:providesPod`, `:providesApp`, and `:usesApp`.
*   **C2 (Metadata about Solid infrastructure):** Encoded via the `:Pod`, `:SolidSpecification`, and `:Functionality` classes, linked by the `:runsOnSpecification` and `:hasInstalledFunctionality` object properties.
*   **C3 (Availability/Discovery of categories of data):** Encoded via the `:Data` and `:DataCategory` classes, linked by `:hasDataCategory`. The location aspect is captured by the `:isStoredIn` property linking `:Data` to a `:Pod`.
*   **C4 (Data handling practices):** Encoded via the `:PrivacyNotice` class and the `:providesPrivacyNotice` property. The declaration of what data is needed is captured by the `:requiresData` property linking to `:DataCategory`.
*   **C5 (Users cannot express their privacy policies):** Encoded via the `:PrivacyPolicy`, `:DataRequest`, and `:DataDecision` classes, linked to the `:User` via `:expressesPolicy`, `:managesRequest`, and `:makesDecision`.
*   **C6 (No logging or record-keeping):** Encoded via the `:Log`, `:ConsentRecord`, and `:Action` classes. The relationships are captured by `:recordsAction`, `:performsAction`, and `:actsOnData`.
*   **C7 (No legal compliance checks):** Encoded via the `:GDPRRight`, `:AuditingInformation`, and `:Authority` classes, supported by the `:exercisesRight` and `:providesAuditingInfo` properties.

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping

*   **What type of action, e.g., create, update, erase, is being performed on the data?**
    *   *Classes:* `:Action`, `:Data`
    *   *Properties:* `:actsOnData` (ObjectProperty), `:actionType` (DatatypeProperty with `xsd:string` range to capture "create", "update", etc.)
*   **Who is the entity interacting with the data?**
    *   *Classes:* `:SolidActor`, `:Data`
    *   *Properties:* `:interactsWithData` (ObjectProperty)
*   **Who is the entity publishing the log?**
    *   *Classes:* `:SolidActor`, `:Log`
    *   *Properties:* `:publishesLog` (ObjectProperty)
*   **When was the log issued?**
    *   *Classes:* `:Log`
    *   *Properties:* `:issuedAt` (DatatypeProperty with `xsd:dateTime` range)
*   **Where is the data being stored?**
    *   *Classes:* `:Data`, `:Pod`
    *   *Properties:* `:isStoredIn` (ObjectProperty)
*   **What application/service is being used to generate the data, if any?**
    *   *Classes:* `:Application`, `:Data`
    *   *Properties:* `:generatesData` (ObjectProperty)

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts

*   **"Machine-readable" (from C4):** Excluded as a distinct class or property. Machine-readability is an implementation characteristic of how the `:PrivacyNotice` is serialized (e.g., as RDF/JSON-LD), rather than a structural ontological concept.
*   **"Granular access" (from C3):** Excluded as a standalone class. Granular access is a system capability enabled by the existence of `:DataCategory` and `:DataDecision`, rather than an entity itself.
*   **Specific GDPR Rights (e.g., "Withdrawing consent" from C7):** Excluded as individual subclasses. To keep the ODP generalized and avoid over-engineering, this is modeled broadly under the `:GDPRRight` class and can be instantiated as specific individuals or captured via `:Action` types in a populated knowledge graph.
