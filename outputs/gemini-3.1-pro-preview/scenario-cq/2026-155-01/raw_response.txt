```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
    rdfs:label "Cultural Heritage Survey Ontology Design Pattern" ;
    rdfs:comment "An ODP modeling the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities, including observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes." .

### Classes ###

:AcquisitionActivity a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Acquisition Activity" ;
    rdfs:comment "An activity focused on acquiring data or observations during a survey." .

:AcquisitionParameter a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Acquisition Parameter" ;
    rdfs:comment "A parameter or setting (e.g., exposure time, ISO, aperture) that characterizes an acquisition cycle." .

:CulturalProperty a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Cultural Property" ;
    rdfs:comment "A cultural heritage entity that is the subject of a survey." .

:Expert a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Expert" ;
    rdfs:comment "A person with specialized knowledge participating in a survey." .

:FeatureOfInterest a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Feature of Interest" ;
    rdfs:comment "A specific feature or part of a cultural property being investigated." .

:Hardware a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Hardware" ;
    rdfs:comment "Physical equipment used to conduct a survey." .

:Interpretation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Interpretation" ;
    rdfs:comment "An interpretation or claim supported by observations." .

:Method a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Method" ;
    rdfs:comment "A method or investigation technique adopted in a survey." .

:Observation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "An observation produced during a survey or investigation activity." .

:Participation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Participation" ;
    rdfs:comment "An n-ary relationship class representing the involvement of an expert in a survey under a specific role." .

:PhotographicAcquisitionCycle a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:subClassOf :AcquisitionActivity ;
    rdfs:label "Photographic Acquisition Cycle" ;
    rdfs:comment "A specific type of acquisition activity involving photographic capture within a survey." .

:PostProcessedOutput a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Post-processed Output" ;
    rdfs:comment "Outputs such as interactive images, spectra, or datasets derived from acquisition or observation activities." .

:Role a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Role" ;
    rdfs:comment "The specific function or position an expert assumes during their participation in a survey." .

:Software a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Software" ;
    rdfs:comment "Computer programs or applications used to conduct a survey." .

:Survey a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Survey" ;
    rdfs:comment "A scientific survey or investigation activity performed on cultural heritage entities." .

:TechnicalConfiguration a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Technical Configuration" ;
    rdfs:comment "The technical setup or configuration used to conduct a survey." .

### Object Properties ###

:derivedFrom a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "derived from" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a post-processed output to the underlying acquisition activity or observation it was created from." ;
    rdfs:domain :PostProcessedOutput ;
    rdfs:range [ a owl:Class ;
                 owl:unionOf ( :AcquisitionActivity :Observation ) ] .

:hasConfiguration a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has configuration" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a survey to the technical configuration used to conduct it." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :TechnicalConfiguration .

:hasParameter a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has parameter" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a photographic acquisition cycle to its characterizing acquisition parameters." ;
    rdfs:domain :PhotographicAcquisitionCycle ;
    rdfs:range :AcquisitionParameter .

:hasParticipant a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has participant" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a participation context to the expert involved." ;
    rdfs:domain :Participation ;
    rdfs:range :Expert .

:hasParticipation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has participation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a survey to the participation context of experts." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :Participation .

:hasRole a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has role" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a participation context to the role assumed by the expert." ;
    rdfs:domain :Participation ;
    rdfs:range :Role .

:investigates a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "investigates" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a survey to the cultural property or feature of interest being investigated." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range [ a owl:Class ;
                 owl:unionOf ( :CulturalProperty :FeatureOfInterest ) ] .

:isPartOf a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "is part of" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates an entity (like an acquisition cycle) to the broader survey it belongs to." ;
    rdfs:domain :PhotographicAcquisitionCycle ;
    rdfs:range :Survey .

:producesObservation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "produces observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a survey or investigation activity to the observations it produces." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :Observation .

:supportsInterpretation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "supports interpretation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links an observation to the interpretation or claim it supports." ;
    rdfs:domain :Observation ;
    rdfs:range :Interpretation .

:usesHardware a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "uses hardware" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a survey to the hardware used to conduct it." ;

:usesMethod a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "uses method" ;

    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :Method .

:usesSoftware a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "uses software" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a survey to the software used to conduct it." ;
    rdfs:domain :Survey ;
    rdfs:range :Software .
```

### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping
- **"models the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities"**: Encoded by the classes `:Survey` and `:CulturalProperty`, and the object property `:investigates`.
- **"represents the relationships between surveys, observations..."**: Encoded by the class `:Observation` and the property `:producesObservation` linking `:Survey` to `:Observation`.
- **"...methods..."**: Encoded by the class `:Method` and the property `:usesMethod`.
- **"...equipment configurations..."**: Encoded by the classes `:Hardware`, `:Software`, and `:TechnicalConfiguration`, linked to `:Survey` via `:usesHardware`, `:usesSoftware`, and `:hasConfiguration`.
- **"...and interpretation processes."**: Encoded by the class `:Interpretation` and the property `:supportsInterpretation` linking `:Observation` to `:Interpretation`.

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping
- **CQ1 (Which survey... performed on a given cultural property or... feature of interest?)**: Answered using `:Survey`, `:CulturalProperty`, `:FeatureOfInterest`, and the `:investigates` property.
- **CQ2 (Which method... was adopted in a specific survey...?)**: Answered using `:Survey`, `:Method`, and the `:usesMethod` property.
- **CQ3 (Which observations were produced during a given survey...?)**: Answered using `:Survey`, `:Observation`, and the `:producesObservation` property.
- **CQ4 (Which experts participated... and with which roles?)**: Answered using the n-ary relation pattern via the `:Participation` class. `:Survey` is linked to `:Participation` via `:hasParticipation`; `:Participation` links to `:Expert` via `:hasParticipant` and to `:Role` via `:hasRole`.
- **CQ5 (Which hardware, software, and technical configurations were used...?)**: Answered using `:Survey`, `:Hardware`, `:Software`, `:TechnicalConfiguration`, and the properties `:usesHardware`, `:usesSoftware`, and `:hasConfiguration`.
- **CQ6 (What interpretations or claims are supported by the observations...?)**: Answered using `:Observation`, `:Interpretation`, and the `:supportsInterpretation` property.
- **CQ7 (How are post-processed outputs... related to the underlying acquisition or observation activities?)**: Answered using `:PostProcessedOutput`, `:AcquisitionActivity`, `:Observation`, and the `:derivedFrom` property.
- **CQ8 (Which acquisition parameters... characterised a specific photographic acquisition cycle within a survey?)**: Answered using `:PhotographicAcquisitionCycle` (linked to `:Survey` via `:isPartOf`), `:AcquisitionParameter`, and the `:hasParameter` property.

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts
- **Specific Post-processed Outputs (interactive images, spectra, datasets)**: Excluded as distinct schema classes. These are provided as examples ("e.g.") in the CQs and are better modeled as instances or specific subclasses of `:PostProcessedOutput` in a domain-specific implementation rather than cluttering the core ODP.
- **Specific Acquisition Parameters (exposure time, ISO, aperture)**: Excluded as distinct schema classes for the same reason. They are examples of the broader `:AcquisitionParameter` class and should be instantiated or subclassed based on the specific photographic equipment being modeled.
- **"Investigation Activity" / "Investigation Technique" / "Claim"**: Excluded as separate classes to avoid redundancy. They are treated as synonymous with `:Survey`, `:Method`, and `:Interpretation` respectively, as implied by the "or" phrasing in the CQs (e.g., "survey or investigation activity").
