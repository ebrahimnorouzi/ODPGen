:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;

    rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern representing pediatric asthma patients, their symptoms, medication intake, outdoor environmental allergens, and weighted causal relations between these factors." .

:Patient a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Patient" ;
    rdfs:comment "A pediatric asthma patient participating in the continuous self-monitoring project." .

    rdfs:label "Asthma Symptom" ;
    rdfs:comment "A symptom experienced by an asthma patient." .

    rdfs:subClassOf :AsthmaSymptom ;
    rdfs:label "Cough" ;
    rdfs:comment "A cough symptom experienced by the patient." .

    rdfs:subClassOf :AsthmaSymptom ;
    rdfs:label "Chest Tightness" ;
    rdfs:comment "A chest tightness symptom experienced by the patient." .

    rdfs:subClassOf :AsthmaSymptom ;

    rdfs:label "Medication Intake" ;
    rdfs:comment "The event or record of a patient taking asthma medication." .

:hasEffectWeight a owl:DatatypeProperty ;

### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping

*   **"Asthma patients"**: Encoded as `:Patient a owl:Class`.
*   **"Symptoms, such as cough, chest tightness, wheezing, and breathing patterns"**: Encoded as `:AsthmaSymptom a owl:Class` with specific subclasses `:Cough`, `:ChestTightness`, `:Wheezing`, and `:BreathingPattern`. The relationship to the patient is captured by `:experiencesSymptom`.
*   **"Intake of medications"**: Encoded as `:MedicationIntake a owl:Class` and linked to the patient via `:hasMedicationIntake`.
*   **"Outdoor environment features, such as pollen and air quality index (AQI)" / "presence of outdoor allergen: pollen/AQI"**: Encoded as `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen a owl:Class` with subclasses `:Pollen` and `:AQI`. The patient's exposure is captured by `:isExposedTo`.
*   **"The edges represent the causal relations with effect weights"**: Because standard OWL binary properties cannot hold attributes (like weights), this is encoded using an N-ary relation pattern (reification). It is represented by `:CausalRelation a owl:Class`, which connects to the cause via `:hasCause`, the effect via `:hasEffect`, and holds the weight via `:hasEffectWeight`.

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping

*   **CQ1: Is the medication intake by the patient caused by the presence of outdoor environmental allergen such as pollen or AQI?**
    *   *Classes/Properties:* `:MedicationIntake`, `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen` (and subclasses `:Pollen`, `:AQI`), `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`.
    *   *Mechanism:* Query for a `:CausalRelation` where `:hasCause` is an instance of `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen` and `:hasEffect` is an instance of `:MedicationIntake`.
*   **CQ2: How much does the presence of an asthma symptom effect the intake of medication?**
    *   *Classes/Properties:* `:AsthmaSymptom`, `:MedicationIntake`, `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`, `:hasEffectWeight`.
    *   *Mechanism:* Query the `:hasEffectWeight` of a `:CausalRelation` linking an `:AsthmaSymptom` (cause) to `:MedicationIntake` (effect).
*   **CQ3: How does an outdoor environmental allergen affect the patient?**
    *   *Classes/Properties:* `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen`, `:Patient`, `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`, `:experiencesSymptom`, `:hasMedicationIntake`.
    *   *Mechanism:* Query for `:CausalRelation` instances where `:hasCause` is the allergen, and check the `:hasEffect` (which will map to symptoms or medication intake associated with the `:Patient`).
*   **CQ4: What is the probable cause of the cough experienced by the patient?**
    *   *Classes/Properties:* `:Cough`, `:Patient`, `:experiencesSymptom`, `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`.
    *   *Mechanism:* Query for `:CausalRelation` instances where `:hasEffect` is `:Cough` to retrieve the corresponding `:hasCause`.
*   **CQ5: What if pollen (or AQI) allergen was not present in the outdoor environment, how would it effect the intake of medication?**
    *   *Classes/Properties:* `:Pollen`, `:AQI`, `:MedicationIntake`, `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`, `:hasEffectWeight`.
    *   *Mechanism:* While OWL cannot natively compute counterfactual math, the schema structurally supports an external reasoning engine by providing the exact causal pathways and weights (`:hasEffectWeight`) between `:Pollen`/`:AQI` and `:MedicationIntake` needed to calculate the "what if" scenario.
*   **CQ6: What if the patient did not experience a cough, how would it effect the intake of medication?**
    *   *Classes/Properties:* `:Cough`, `:MedicationIntake`, `:CausalRelation`, `:hasCause`, `:hasEffect`, `:hasEffectWeight`.
    *   *Mechanism:* Similar to CQ5, the schema provides the structural causal link and weight between `:Cough` and `:MedicationIntake` required to simulate the absence of the symptom.

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts

*   **"kHealth-Asthma project" / "continuous self-monitoring project"**: Excluded because these represent administrative metadata about the study itself, rather than the domain knowledge required to answer the competency questions regarding causal medical relationships.
*   **"Pediatric"**: While the scenario mentions pediatric patients, the CQs only ask about "the patient". Creating a specific `PediatricPatient` subclass was excluded to avoid over-engineering, as age demographics are not queried in the CQs.
