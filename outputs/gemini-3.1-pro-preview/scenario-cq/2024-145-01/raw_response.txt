```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
    rdfs:label "Legal Court and Bad Check Ontology" ;
    rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern capturing court hierarchies and bad check crime regulations." .

# --- Classes ---

:Court a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Court" ;
    rdfs:comment "A tribunal presided over by a judge, judges, or a magistrate in civil and criminal cases." .

:DistrictCourt a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:subClassOf :Court ;
    rdfs:label "District Court" ;
    rdfs:comment "A court that has jurisdiction over a specific district." .

:State a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "State" ;
    rdfs:comment "A principal administrative division of a country." .

:District a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "District" ;
    rdfs:comment "An administrative division of a state." .

:Check a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Check" ;
    rdfs:comment "A written, dated, and signed instrument that directs a bank to pay a specific sum of money to the bearer." .

:BadCheck a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:subClassOf :Check ;
    rdfs:label "Bad Check" ;
    rdfs:comment "A check drawn on a nonexistent account or on an account with insufficient funds." .

:CheckTransactionType a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Check Transaction Type" ;
    rdfs:comment "The type or category of a check transaction." .

:CheckPayment a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Check Payment" ;
    rdfs:comment "The event or record of a payment made using a check." .

:TransactionStatus a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Transaction Status" ;
    rdfs:comment "The current status of a check payment transaction." .

# --- Object Properties ---

:locatedInState a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "located in state" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates an entity, such as a district or district court, to the state it is located in." ;
    rdfs:range :State .

:hasAuthorityOver a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has authority over" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a higher court to a lower court over which it has jurisdiction or authority." ;
    rdfs:domain :Court ;
    rdfs:range :Court .

:hasTransactionType a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has transaction type" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a check (such as a bad check) to its transaction type." ;
    rdfs:domain :Check ;
    rdfs:range :CheckTransactionType .

:isPaymentOf a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "is payment of" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a check payment event to the specific check it pertains to." ;
    rdfs:domain :CheckPayment ;
    rdfs:range :Check .

:hasTransactionStatus a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:label "has transaction status" ;
    rdfs:comment "Relates a check payment to its current transaction status." ;
    rdfs:domain :CheckPayment ;
    rdfs:range :TransactionStatus .

# --- Datatype Properties ---

:paymentDate a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
    rdfs:label "payment date" ;
    rdfs:comment "The date and time when a check payment occurred." ;
    rdfs:domain :CheckPayment ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime .
```

### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping
*   **"captures the court hierarchies"**: Encoded via the `:Court` class, its subclass `:DistrictCourt`, and the recursive object property `:hasAuthorityOver` (domain and range of `:Court`).
*   **"bad check crime related regulations"**: Encoded via the `:BadCheck` class (subclass of `:Check`), `:CheckPayment`, `:CheckTransactionType`, `:TransactionStatus`, and their connecting properties (`:hasTransactionType`, `:isPaymentOf`, `:hasTransactionStatus`, `:paymentDate`).

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping
*   **List all district courts in a given state.**
    *   Classes: `:DistrictCourt`, `:State`
    *   Properties: `:locatedInState`
*   **In which state is a given district court instance located?**
    *   Classes: `:DistrictCourt`, `:State`
    *   Properties: `:locatedInState`
*   **How many districts are in a given state of India?**
    *   Classes: `:District`, `:State`
    *   Properties: `:locatedInState`
*   **Which court has authority over a given court?**
    *   Classes: `:Court`
    *   Properties: `:hasAuthorityOver`
*   **What is the check transaction type for a bad check?**
    *   Classes: `:BadCheck`, `:CheckTransactionType`
    *   Properties: `:hasTransactionType`
*   **When is a check payment of a bad check?**
    *   Classes: `:CheckPayment`, `:BadCheck`
    *   Properties: `:isPaymentOf` (to link the payment to the bad check), `:paymentDate` (to answer "When")
*   **What is the transaction status of a given check payment?**
    *   Classes: `:CheckPayment`, `:TransactionStatus`
    *   Properties: `:hasTransactionStatus`

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts
*   **"India"**: Excluded because it represents a specific instance of a country/state context rather than a schema-level class. The schema supports this via the generic `:State` class.
*   **"Legal tasks", "complex information", "primarily unstructured in nature"**: Excluded because these describe the operational context, system requirements, or data formats rather than the structural domain entities (courts, checks, locations) needed to answer the competency questions.
*   **"Regulations"**: While mentioned in the scenario text, the competency questions focus strictly on the operational data (checks, payments, statuses, courts) rather than modeling the legal texts, statutes, or regulations themselves. Adding a `Regulation` class would violate the requirement to not add concepts ungrounded in the CQs.
