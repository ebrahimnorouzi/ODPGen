### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping
*   **"Knowledge representing real-world information aiding the evaluation of ODRL Policies."**: Encoded via the `:Policy` class.
*   **"Bob wants to have access..."**: Encoded via the `:Party` class (representing Bob) and the `:Action` class (representing access).
*   **"...to a certain video stream..."**: Encoded via the `:Asset` class and the `:targetsAsset` object property linking the action to the asset.
*   **"...but only needs access to minutes 4 to 9..."**: Encoded via the `:TemporalEntity` class and the `:hasTemporalConstraint` object property linking the action to the specific time interval.
*   **"...for the purposes of an academic research project."**: Encoded via the `:Purpose` class and the `:hasPurpose` object property linking the action to its intent.

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping
*   **What is the current time?**: Enabled by the `:currentTime` datatype property.
*   **What is the location of a party?**: Enabled by the `:Party` and `:Location` classes, and the `:hasLocation` object property.
*   **What assets are part of the asset collection of the policy that is being evaluated?**: Enabled by the `:Policy`, `:AssetCollection`, and `:Asset` classes, along with the `:hasAssetCollection` and `:hasAsset` object properties.
*   **Which parties are part of the party collection of the policy that is being evaluated?**: Enabled by the `:Policy`, `:PartyCollection`, and `:Party` classes, along with the `:hasPartyCollection` and `:hasParty` object properties.
*   **What actions were already performed or attempted?**: Enabled by the `:Action` class and the `:actionStatus` datatype property (which captures states like "performed" or "attempted").
*   **How many times has a rule been exercised?**: Enabled by the `:Rule` class and the `:exerciseCount` datatype property.
*   **What information is available about an event that has occurred?**: Enabled by the `:Event` class and the `:eventInformation` datatype property.
*   **How long has a rule been exercised?**: Enabled by the `:Rule` class and the `:exerciseDuration` datatype property.
*   **Which recipients or categories of recipients can receive the result of the rule that has been exercised?**: Enabled by the `:Rule`, `:Recipient`, and `:RecipientCategory` classes, along with the `:hasRecipient` and `:hasRecipientCategory` object properties.
*   **Has a financial payment been made? What was the amount paid?**: Enabled by the `:Payment` class (subclass of `:Event`), and the `:isPaymentMade` and `:paymentAmount` datatype properties.

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts
*   **Specific Instances (Bob, Video Stream, Minutes 4 to 9, Academic Research Project)**: Excluded to comply with the strict requirement of outputting schema axioms only (no A-Box individuals). These are generalized into `:Party`, `:Asset`, `:TemporalEntity`, and `:Purpose`.
*   **ODRL Core Subclasses (Permission, Prohibition, Duty)**: Excluded because the scenario and CQs only explicitly mention "Policy" and "Rule". Adding specific ODRL rule types would violate the requirement to not add concepts ungrounded in the provided text.
*   **Rule Results**: While CQ9 mentions "receive the result of the rule", a dedicated `RuleResult` class was excluded. The relationship is modeled directly from the `:Rule` to the `:Recipient` / `:RecipientCategory` to keep the ontology structurally minimal while fully answering the CQ.
