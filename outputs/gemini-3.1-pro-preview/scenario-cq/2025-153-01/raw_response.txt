```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#> .

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
    rdfs:label "Temporal Indirection Ontology Design Pattern" ;
    rdfs:comment "An ontology design pattern representing temporal indirection, where persistent identifiers dynamically resolve to different referents over time through temporal bindings, transitions, and rules." .

### Classes ###

:PersistentIdentifier a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Persistent Identifier" ;
    rdfs:comment "A stable, invariant referent or symbol (e.g., 'CL1', 'Primary Oncologist Ward 7') that provides continuity across successive underlying entities." .

:Referent a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Referent" ;
    rdfs:comment "The specific, underlying entity (e.g., February 2025 contract, specific physician, specific sensor) that the persistent identifier resolves to at a given time." .

:TemporalBinding a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Temporal Binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "A reified relationship representing the period during which a Persistent Identifier resolves to a specific Referent." .

:Transition a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Transition" ;
    rdfs:comment "An event representing the rollover or change from one Temporal Binding to another for a given Persistent Identifier." .

:Rule a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Rule" ;
    rdfs:comment "The condition, policy, or trigger (e.g., contract expiration, shift change) that causes a Transition to occur." .

:Observation a owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Real-time attributes, measurements, or events (e.g., last trade price, calibration) bound to a specific Referent." .

### Object Properties ###

:hasBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :PersistentIdentifier ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:label "has binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a persistent identifier to its temporal bindings over time." .

:bindsTo a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :Referent ;
    rdfs:label "binds to" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a temporal binding to the specific referent active during that period." .

:hasObservation a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Referent ;
    rdfs:range :Observation ;
    rdfs:label "has observation" ;
    rdfs:comment "Associates real-time attributes or observations with the specific referent instance." .

:startsBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Transition ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:label "starts binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Indicates the new temporal binding that becomes active as a result of a transition." .

:endsBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Transition ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:label "ends binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Indicates the previous temporal binding that is terminated by a transition." .

:triggeredBy a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Transition ;
    rdfs:range :Rule ;
    rdfs:label "triggered by" ;
    rdfs:comment "Links a transition event to the rule or condition that caused it." .

:nextBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:label "next binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Establishes the sequence of bindings by linking to the chronologically subsequent binding." .

:previousBinding a owl:ObjectProperty ;
    owl:inverseOf :nextBinding ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:label "previous binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "Establishes the sequence of bindings by linking to the chronologically preceding binding." .

### Datatype Properties ###

:hasStartTime a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime ;
    rdfs:label "has start time" ;
    rdfs:comment "The timestamp when a temporal binding becomes active." .

:hasEndTime a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime ;
    rdfs:label "has end time" ;
    rdfs:comment "The timestamp when a temporal binding ceases to be active." .

:isCurrentBinding a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :TemporalBinding ;
    rdfs:range xsd:boolean ;
    rdfs:label "is current binding" ;
    rdfs:comment "A boolean flag indicating whether the temporal binding is the currently active one." .

:transitionTime a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Transition ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime ;
    rdfs:label "transition time" ;
    rdfs:comment "The exact timestamp when the transition between referents occurred." .

:observationTime a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Observation ;
    rdfs:range xsd:dateTime ;
    rdfs:label "observation time" ;
    rdfs:comment "The timestamp at which an observation or attribute measurement was recorded." .
```

### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping

*   **Requirement: Persistent identifiers serve as stable referents.**
    *   *Axioms*: Declaration of `:PersistentIdentifier`.
*   **Requirement: Identifiers dynamically resolve to specific entities (e.g., front-month contract, specific physician).**
    *   *Axioms*: Declaration of `:Referent`, `:TemporalBinding`, and the object properties `:hasBinding` and `:bindsTo`. This reification allows the identifier to map to different referents over time.
*   **Requirement: Continuity across successive rollovers/transitions.**
    *   *Axioms*: Declaration of `:Transition`, `:startsBinding`, `:endsBinding`, `:nextBinding`, and `:previousBinding`.
*   **Requirement: Real-time attributes (price, volume, etc.) are bound to the current contract instance.**
    *   *Axioms*: Declaration of `:Observation` and the object property `:hasObservation` (domain: `:Referent`, range: `:Observation`).
*   **Requirement: Transitions are triggered by rules (e.g., contract approaches expiration).**
    *   *Axioms*: Declaration of `:Rule` and the object property `:triggeredBy` (domain: `:Transition`, range: `:Rule`).

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping

*   **CQ1: Which entity does a persistent identifier currently reference?**
    *   *Enabled by*: `:PersistentIdentifier` -> `:hasBinding` -> `:TemporalBinding` -> `:bindsTo` -> `:Referent`, filtered by `:isCurrentBinding` (boolean) or checking if `:hasEndTime` is empty/in the future.
*   **CQ2: Which entity was referenced at a past timestamp?**
    *   *Enabled by*: `:TemporalBinding` -> `:bindsTo` -> `:Referent`, filtered by checking if the target timestamp falls between `:hasStartTime` and `:hasEndTime`.
*   **CQ3: When did referent transitions occur?**
    *   *Enabled by*: `:Transition` class and its `:transitionTime` datatype property.
*   **CQ4: What observations are associated with specific referents?**
    *   *Enabled by*: `:Referent` -> `:hasObservation` -> `:Observation`.
*   **CQ5: What is the full sequence of referents over time?**
    *   *Enabled by*: `:nextBinding` and `:previousBinding` properties linking `:TemporalBinding` instances in a chronological chain.
*   **CQ6: Which rules triggered specific transitions?**
    *   *Enabled by*: `:Transition` -> `:triggeredBy` -> `:Rule`.
*   **CQ7: Are there gaps or overlaps in temporal bindings?**
    *   *Enabled by*: Comparing the `:hasEndTime` of a `:TemporalBinding` with the `:hasStartTime` of its `:nextBinding`.
*   **CQ8: Can complete context be rebuilt for any time point?**
    *   *Enabled by*: The comprehensive temporal scoping provided by `:hasStartTime`, `:hasEndTime`, `:transitionTime`, and `:observationTime` across all core entities.

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts

*   **Domain-Specific Subclasses (e.g., `FuturesContract`, `Physician`, `Sensor`, `FlightNumber`)**: Excluded because the prompt requests an Ontology Design Pattern (ODP) for the *paradigmatic example of temporal indirection*. Including domain-specific classes would limit the pattern's reusability across the multiple domains mentioned in the scenario.
*   **Specific Observation Types (e.g., `Price`, `Volume`, `Calibration`)**: Abstracted into the generic `:Observation` class. The scenario uses these merely as examples of "real-time attributes".
*   **Direct Property from Identifier to Observation**: The scenario states observations are "exposed through the invariant identifier" but "bound to the current contract instance". To maintain semantic faithfulness and avoid redundancy, observations are strictly bound to the `:Referent`. Exposure through the identifier is a query-level traversal (`Identifier` -> `Current Binding` -> `Referent` -> `Observation`) rather than a direct structural property.
