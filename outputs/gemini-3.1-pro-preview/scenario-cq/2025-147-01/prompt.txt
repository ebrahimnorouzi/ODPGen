Scenario:
In the maritime domain, a wide range of heterogeneous data sources, like AIS tracks, technical ship specifications, and registry databases, must be combined and analyzed to support decision-making processes in logistics, fleet management, monitoring, and research. However, these datasets often lack a unified semantic structure as they are available in different formats that are not compatible, which limits interoperability, reuse, and automated reasoning. It should provide a formalized and extensible conceptual model for representing ships and their properties in a machine-interpretable way.

Four specific use cases are described: (1) Fleet Management and Operational Planning — standardized and machine-readable descriptions of vessel characteristics support better integration of heterogeneous fleet data; (2) Search and Rescue (SAR) Operations — enabling fast semantic querying of relevant vessel trajectory data for time-critical emergency situations; (3) Semantic Data Integration, Search and Analysis — acting as a common semantic layer across disparate data systems such as AIS feeds, ship registries, classification society databases, and onboard monitoring platforms; (4) Digital Twins and Life Cycle Simulations — providing a structural backbone for building digital twins of vessels by modeling the types of propulsion systems and their interconnections.
