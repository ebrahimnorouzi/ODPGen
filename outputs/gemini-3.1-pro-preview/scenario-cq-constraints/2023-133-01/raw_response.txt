**a) Requirements from the scenario that are covered:**

- **Directed edges representing causal relations:** Covered by the reified `:CausalRelation` class, linked via `:hasSourceEvent` and `:hasTargetEvent`.
- **Effect weights:** Covered by the `:hasEffectWeight` datatype property attached to the reified causal relation.
- **Mediated causal relations:** Covered by the transitive `:causes` object property, allowing reasoning over indirect causes (e.g., rain -> wet -> slippery).

**b) Scenario concepts explicitly excluded and why:**

- **Probabilistic/Bayesian mathematical calculations:** Excluded because OWL is a Description Logic language designed for structural and semantic reasoning, not for executing mathematical probability calculations. The ontology captures the *structure* and *weights* of the network, leaving the actual Bayesian computation to an external reasoning engine.
