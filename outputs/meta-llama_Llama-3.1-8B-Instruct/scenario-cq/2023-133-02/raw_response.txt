### Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping

1. "Asthma is a complex disease with multiple factors affecting a patient's condition."
   - Axiom: `:AsthmaPatient a owl:Class ;`
   - This axiom represents the concept of an asthma patient, which is a key entity in the scenario.

2. "The data collected about asthma patients include their (1) symptoms, such as cough, chest tightness, wheezing, and breathing patterns, (2) intake of medications, and (3) outdoor environment features, such as pollen and air quality index (AQI)."
   - Axioms:
     - `:AsthmaSymptom a owl:Class ;`
     - `:Medication a owl:Class ;`
     - `:OutdoorEnvironmentalAllergen a owl:Class ;`
   - These axioms represent the concepts of asthma symptoms, medications, and outdoor environmental allergens, which are essential entities in the scenario.

3. "The edges represent the causal relations with effect weights."
   - Axioms:
     - `:causes a owl:ObjectProperty ;`
     - `:effectWeight a owl:DatatypeProperty ;`
   - These axioms represent the causal relations between outdoor environmental allergens and asthma symptoms, along with the effect weights.
