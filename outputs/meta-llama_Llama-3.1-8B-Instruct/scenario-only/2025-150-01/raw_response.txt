**Documentation**

### Intent
This ontology design pattern models the structure of Message Queuing Telemetry Transport (MQTT) messages, including their formats and encodings, to enable seamless alignment between observation and actuation process descriptions with transport semantics.

### Covered requirements
* Modeling MQTT message protocols for automated process data
* Capturing various payload formats and encodings used in MQTT message protocols
* Enabling seamless alignment between observation and actuation process descriptions with transport semantics

### Known assumptions and exclusions
* This ontology assumes that the context of the EKI project is relevant to the modeling of MQTT message protocols.
* This ontology does not cover the specifics of the EKI project, which may be modeled separately.
* This ontology does not include concepts not implied by the scenario, such as the specifics of the MQTT protocol or the context of the EKI project.
