**Documentation**

### Intent
The Cultural Heritage Survey Ontology models the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities, representing relationships between surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes.

### Covered requirements
* Models the structure of scientific survey activities performed on cultural heritage entities
* Represents relationships between surveys, observations, methods, equipment configurations, and interpretation processes

### Known assumptions and exclusions
* Assumes that a survey activity is performed on a single cultural heritage entity
* Excludes modeling of relationships between surveys and other external entities (e.g., people, organizations)
* Excludes modeling of survey activity metadata (e.g., date, location, duration)
* Excludes modeling of equipment configuration details (e.g., specific equipment used, settings)
