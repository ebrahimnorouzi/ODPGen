**Documentation**

### Intent
This ontology design pattern models the governance challenges in Solid apps, as identified in the paper.

### Covered requirements
* Identify 7 challenges motivating the need for governance patterns in Solid apps
* Model each challenge as a distinct class
* Define a property to relate a challenge to its identifier
* Establish subclass relationships between challenges
* Declare disjointness between challenges

### Known assumptions and exclusions
* This ontology assumes that the challenges are distinct and unrelated, and thus declares disjointness between them.
* This ontology does not include any additional concepts or relationships not implied by the scenario.
* This ontology does not provide any additional information about the challenges beyond their identifiers and descriptions.
