```turtle
@prefix owl:  <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#>.
@prefix rdf:  <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#>.
@prefix rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>.
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#>.
@prefix :     <http://example.org/odp#>.

:Ontology a owl:Ontology ;
  rdfs:label "Ship Properties Ontology" ;
  rdfs:comment "Formalized conceptual model for representing ships and their properties.".

:Ship a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Ship" ;
  rdfs:comment "A vessel with specific characteristics.".

:VesselCharacteristics a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Vessel Characteristics" ;
  rdfs:comment "Standardized and machine-readable descriptions of vessel characteristics.".

:ShipType a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Ship Type" ;
  rdfs:comment "Classification of ships based on their design and purpose.".

:PropulsionSystem a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:label "Propulsion System" ;
  rdfs:comment "Type of system used to generate thrust for a vessel.".

:Ship a owl:disjointWith :VesselCharacteristics ;
  owl:disjointWith :ShipType ;
  owl:disjointWith :PropulsionSystem.

:hasShipType a owl:Property ;
  rdfs:label "has Ship Type" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relationship between a ship and its classification." ;
  rdfs:domain :Ship ;
  rdfs:range :ShipType.

:hasPropulsionSystem a owl:Property ;
  rdfs:label "has Propulsion System" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relationship between a ship and its propulsion system." ;
  rdfs:domain :Ship ;
  rdfs:range :PropulsionSystem.

:hasVesselCharacteristics a owl:Property ;
  rdfs:label "has Vessel Characteristics" ;
  rdfs:comment "Relationship between a ship and its standardized and machine-readable descriptions." ;
  rdfs:domain :Ship ;
  rdfs:range :VesselCharacteristics.
```

**Documentation**

### Intent
This ontology design pattern models a formalized conceptual model for representing ships and their properties in a machine-interpretable way, enabling interoperability, reuse, and automated reasoning across disparate data systems.

### Covered requirements
* Formalized conceptual model for representing ships and their properties
* Unified semantic structure for heterogeneous data sources
* Interoperability, reuse, and automated reasoning across disparate data systems
* Standardized and machine-readable descriptions of vessel characteristics
* Classification of ships based on their design and purpose
* Types of propulsion systems and their interconnections

### Known assumptions and exclusions
* This ontology design pattern assumes that the data sources are primarily related to the maritime domain.
* It excludes concepts not implied by the scenario, such as land-based transportation or non-maritime applications.
* The ontology design pattern does not address the specifics of data integration, search, and analysis, which are mentioned in the scenario as use cases.
