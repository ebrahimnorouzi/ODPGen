Documentation:

Intent: The LyonTech Campus Routing Ontology models the spatial and environmental characteristics of the LyonTech La Doua campus, taking into account user preferences and requirements, to provide accessible and personalized routing recommendations.

Covered Requirements:

* R1: The routing system should characterize spatial areas through relevant contextual properties (e.g. noise level, crowdedness).
* R2: The system should identify and help to avoid high-traffic areas during peak hours.
* R3: The system should be able to locate rest areas along or near to potential routes.
* R4: The system should distinguish between vertical transition types (stairs, elevators, escalators) to support preference and capability-aware routing.
* R5: The system should locate the nearest instance of a desired amenity type (vending machine, toilets, reception board) to the current proposed itinerary.
* R6: The system should characterize spaces based on their aesthetic or comfort-related qualities to enable experience-aware routing.

Known Assumptions and Exclusions:

* The ontology assumes that the user has a known starting point and destination.
* The ontology does not account for dynamic environmental factors, such as weather or lighting, that may affect the user's experience.
* The ontology does not consider the user's social or cultural background, which may influence their preferences or requirements.
* The ontology does not include any specific routing algorithms or decision-making processes, as it focuses on the representation of spatial and environmental information.
