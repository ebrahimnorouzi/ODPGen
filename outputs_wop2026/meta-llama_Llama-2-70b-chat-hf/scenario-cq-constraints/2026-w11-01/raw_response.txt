* No mention of wearable devices or sensors.

The WISDOM ontology addresses the challenge of lacking a unified semantic structure in stress research by providing a formalized and extensible conceptual data model for representing stress conditions, stressors, physiological responses, sensors, and contextual information. It supports tasks that rely on structured, interoperable, and machine-readable stress detection, enabling the semantic annotation of wearable stress datasets, dataset harmonization and comparison, contextual interpretation of physiological observations, and digital twins and condition simulations. The ontology does not include explicit mention of stress detection models or algorithms, specific stress stimuli or response measures, experimental design or protocols, data collection or analysis methods, or wearable devices or sensors.
