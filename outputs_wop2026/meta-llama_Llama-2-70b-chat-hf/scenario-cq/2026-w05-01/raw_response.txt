Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping:

1. Mike needs to solve a task by prompting a foundation model. Thus, he needs a prompting technique suitable for the task.
	* Axiom: :SuitablePromptingTechnique
2. The task involves a specific data modality and is subject to different constraints.
	* Axiom: :SuitableDataModality, :SuitableConstraint
3. Another constraint concerns the model architecture, which depends on the available resources.
	* Axiom: :SuitableModelArchitecture
4. He may also explore model families, variants, and evolutions in order to experiment with prompts.
	* Axiom: :SuitableVariant, :SuitableEvolution
5. Furthermore, he relies on the analysis of prior chats to design the solution.
	* Axiom: :ConversationLength, :PromptFrequency, :ResponseStatistics
