Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping:

1. The software requirement of a software is expressed as a Requirement.
	* Axiom: :softwareRequirement a owl:ObjectProperty.
2. The hardware requirement of a software is expressed as a Requirement.
	* Axiom: :hardwareRequirement a owl:ObjectProperty.
3. The type of a software or hardware resource is described by a ResourceType.
	* Axiom: :resourceType a owl:DatatypeProperty.
4. The version of a software resource is described by a ResourceVersion.
	* Axiom: :resourceVersion a owl:DatatypeProperty.
5. A match between a software and a hardware resource is described by a Match.
	* Axiom: :match a owl:ObjectProperty.

CQ-to-axiom mapping:

1. Which is the (software/hardware) requirement of a software?
	* Axiom: :softwareRequirement a owl:ObjectProperty.
2. Which is the software/hardware required by a software?
	* Axiom: :hardwareRequirement a owl:ObjectProperty.
3. Which is the type of software/hardware required by a software?
	* Axiom: :resourceType a owl:DatatypeProperty.
4. Which is the required (minimum/maximum/exact) value of a property of a software/hardware required by a software?
	* Axiom: :resourceVersion a owl:DatatypeProperty.
5. Which is the required (maximum/minimum/exact) version of a software required by a software?
	* Axiom: :resourceVersion a owl:DatatypeProperty.

Intentionally excluded concepts:

* None.

Note: This ontology design pattern (ODP) focuses on the semantic management of digital assets, specifically software and hardware resources, and their requirements. It does not address other aspects of the SMARTY project, such as quantum-resilient heterogeneous edge computing.
