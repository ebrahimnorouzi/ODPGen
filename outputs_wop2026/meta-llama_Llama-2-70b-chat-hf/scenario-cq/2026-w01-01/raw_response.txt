Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping:

1. "The LCA study was conducted using the EIME software tool, connected to a Bill of Materials describing the electronic components of the prototype."
	* Axiom: :LCAStudy rdfs:subClassOf :Prototype.
2. "Inventory data were sourced from two databases: CODDE (v3.00.000) and NegaOctet."
	* Axiom: :InventoryData rdfs:subClassOf :FlowDataset.
3. "Environmental impact calculations were performed using the Environmental Footprint 3.0 (EF 3.0) methodology."
	* Axiom: :EnvironmentalImpact rdfs:subClassOf :LCAStudy.
4. "The resulting inventory and impact data, together with study metadata including hypotheses, parametrisation, system boundary, and actor information, were extracted and subsequently used as input data for the use case."
	* Axiom: :LCAStudy rdfs:subClassOf :FunctionalUnit.
5. "The LCA study was aimed at evaluating the environmental impact of a Near Memory Computing prototype based on C-SRAM technology."
	* Axiom: :Prototype rdfs:subClassOf :BillOfMaterials.

CQ-to-axiom mapping:

1. "What are the input and output flows of all activities?"
	* Axiom: :InputFlow rdfs:subClassOf :DependencyChain.
	* Axiom: :OutputFlow rdfs:subClassOf :DependencyChain.
2. "What is the dependency chain of activities in a system model?"
	* Axiom: :DependencyChain rdfs:subClassOf :LCAStudy.
3. "Which flow datasets come from database X?"
	* Axiom: :FlowDataset rdfs:subClassOf :InventoryData.
4. "What are the elementary and intermediate flows associated with a given activity?"
	* Axiom: :ElementaryFlow rdfs:subClassOf :FlowDataset.
	* Axiom: :IntermediateFlow rdfs:subClassOf :FlowDataset.
5. "Which flows are classified as resources, emissions, or products?"
	* Axiom: :ResourceFlow rdfs:subClassOf :IntermediateFlow.
	* Axiom: :EmissionFlow rdfs:subClassOf :IntermediateFlow.
	* Axiom: :ProductFlow rdfs:subClassOf :IntermediateFlow.
6. "What is the functional unit, scope, system boundary, and responsible agent of the LCA model?"
	* Axiom: :FunctionalUnit rdfs:subClassOf :LCAStudy.
	* Axiom: :Scope rdfs:subClassOf :LCAStudy.
	* Axiom: :SystemBoundary rdfs:subClassOf :LCAStudy.
	* Axiom: :ResponsibleAgent rdfs:subClassOf :LCAStudy.
7. "What is the impact result of a given activity or flow across impact categories?"
	* Axiom: :ImpactResult rdfs:subClassOf :EnvironmentalImpact.
8. "Which flows contribute significantly (e.g., > 2%) to a given impact category?"
	* Axiom: :Contributor rdfs:subClassOf :ImpactResult.
9. "What are the top contributors to an impact category for a given activity?"
	* Axiom: :TopContributor rdfs:subClassOf :Contributor.
10. "What is the total amount of flow X generated within the system boundary?"
	* Axiom: :TotalFlow rdfs:subClassOf :FlowDataset.

Intentionally excluded concepts:

* None.

Note: This ontology design pattern does not include any instance data, as it is focused on providing a structural framework for answering the competency questions. To answer the CQs, instance data would need to be added to the ontology. Additionally, this pattern does not address all possible aspects of LCA studies, but rather focuses on the concepts and relationships relevant to the given scenario and competency questions.
