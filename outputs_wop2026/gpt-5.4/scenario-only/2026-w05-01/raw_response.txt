2. **Documentation**

**Intent:**  
This pattern models how a person designs a prompting-based solution for a task using foundation models, prompting techniques, task constraints, and prior chat analysis.

**Covered requirements:**
- A person needs to solve a task by prompting a foundation model.
- A task needs a prompting technique suitable for it.
- A task involves a specific data modality.
- A task is subject to constraints.
- A model architecture constraint depends on available resources.
- A person may explore model families, variants, and evolutions to experiment with prompts.
- A person relies on analysis of prior chats to design the solution.
- Prior chat analysis can identify which models are most frequently adopted for different tasks.
- Prior chat analysis can identify which prompting techniques are most frequently adopted for different tasks.
- Prior chat analysis can capture statistics on conversation length.
- Prior chat analysis can support retrieving responses to specific prompts.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- “Mike” is generalized as `:Person`; no individual instances are created.
- The pattern does not model detailed prompt contents, response contents, timestamps, or full conversation sequencing, since these are not explicitly required.
- Model family, variant, and evolution are represented as separate classes without additional hierarchy because the scenario only states that they may be explored.
- Adoption frequency and conversation length are modeled as usage-pattern classes with numeric values, without prescribing a specific statistical method.
- “Suitable for the task” is represented structurally through `:needsPromptingTechnique` rather than with a separate suitability class or rule.
