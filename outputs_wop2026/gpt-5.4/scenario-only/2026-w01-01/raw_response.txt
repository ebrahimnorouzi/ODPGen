2. A short documentation section with:

**Intent:**  
This pattern models an LCA study together with its evaluated prototype, supporting tool and bill of materials, inventory data sources, impact assessment methodology, resulting data, study metadata, and their use as input to a use case.

**Covered requirements:**
- Represent an LCA study.
- Represent the prototype evaluated by the study.
- Represent the technology on which the prototype is based.
- Represent the software tool used to conduct the study.
- Represent the bill of materials connected to the software tool.
- Represent electronic components described by the bill of materials.
- Represent databases from which inventory data were sourced.
- Represent the methodology used for environmental impact calculations.
- Represent resulting inventory data and impact data.
- Represent study metadata.
- Represent hypotheses, parametrisation, system boundary, and actor information as parts of study metadata.
- Represent that extracted inventory data, impact data, and metadata are used as input for a use case.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- The pattern is schema-only and intentionally does not include individuals such as specific study names, tool names, database names, or methodology versions.
- “Extracted” data are modeled through their relation to a use case rather than through a separate extraction event, to keep the pattern minimal.
- Actor information is modeled as metadata content, not as explicit actor persons or organizations, because the scenario mentions only actor information.
- No temporal, quantitative, or calculation-detail structures are added, since they are not explicitly required by the scenario.
- Version information is supported generically with a datatype property, but no specific versioned resources are instantiated.
