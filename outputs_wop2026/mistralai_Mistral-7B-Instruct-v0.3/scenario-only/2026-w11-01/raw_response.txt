    rdfs:range :Sensor ;

    rdfs:domain :SensorMeasurement ;

**2. Documentation**

**Intent:** The WISDOM ontology is a pattern for modeling stress detection, encompassing stressors, stress states, physiological responses, sensor measurements, and contextual factors.

**Covered Requirements:**
- Models stressors, stress states, physiological responses, sensor measurements, and contextual factors.
- Links stressors, stress states, physiological responses, sensors, and contextual information.
- Supports semantic annotation of wearable stress datasets.
- Facilitates dataset harmonization and comparison.
- Enables contextual interpretation of physiological observations.
- Provides a structural backbone for digital twins and condition simulations.

**Known Assumptions and Exclusions:**
- Assumes that stress detection is inherently complex and requires a structured, machine-readable model.
- Assumes that sensor measurements provide only indirect cues to stress.
- Excludes individual instances of classes (e.g., specific subjects, sensors, or measurements).
- Excludes axioms for subclass and disjointness relationships, as these depend on the specific scenario and dataset being modeled.
