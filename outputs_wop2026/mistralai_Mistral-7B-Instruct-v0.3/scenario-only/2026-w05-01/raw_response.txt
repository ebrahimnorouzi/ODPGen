**Intent:** This pattern models prompting techniques for foundation models, their data modalities, constraints, and usage patterns.

**Covered requirements:**
- Prompting technique suitable for the task.
- Specific data modality and constraints for the task.
- Model architecture depending on available resources.
- Model families, variants, and evolutions for experimenting with prompts.
- Analysis of prior chats to design the solution.
- Identification of frequently adopted models and prompting techniques for different tasks.
- Usage patterns, conversation length, and responses to specific prompts.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
- This pattern does not include individual instances (e.g., specific prompts, tasks, or foundation models).
- It assumes that a task can't have multiple model families, variants, evolutions, etc. (disjointness axioms).
