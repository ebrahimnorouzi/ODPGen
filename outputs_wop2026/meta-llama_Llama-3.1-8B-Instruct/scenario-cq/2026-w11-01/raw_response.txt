**Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping:**

1. Developing stress detection models is inherently complex, including modeling aspects regarding stressors, stress states, physiological responses, sensor measurements, and contextual factors.
	* Axioms: `:StressCondition a owl:Class ;`, `:Stressor a owl:Class ;`, `:PhysiologicalResponse a owl:Class ;`, `:SensorMeasurement a owl:Class ;`, `:ContextualFactor a owl:Class ;`
2. A stressor (or stimulus) represents an event, condition, or stimulus that has the potential to induce stress in a subject, but whether it does is highly subjective and may depend on the context.
	* Axioms: `:Stressor a owl:Class ;`, `:ContextualFactor a owl:Class ;`
3. Physiological changes, such as decreased heart rate variability, may reflect stress but can also arise from physical activity.
	* Axioms: `:PhysiologicalResponse a owl:Class ;`, `:PhysicalActivity a owl:Class ;`
4. Sensor measurements, therefore, provide only indirect cues to stress by capturing physiological or behavioral responses.
	* Axioms: `:SensorMeasurement a owl:Class ;`, `:PhysiologicalResponse a owl:Class ;`, `:BehavioralResponse a owl:Class ;`
5. WISDOM is a structured, machine-readable model for physiology-based stress detection by explicitly linking stressors, stress states, physiological responses, sensors, and contextual information.
	* Axioms: `:StressCondition a owl:Class ;`, `:Stressor a owl:Class ;`, `:PhysiologicalResponse a owl:Class ;`, `:Sensor a owl:Class ;`, `:ContextualFactor a owl:Class ;`
