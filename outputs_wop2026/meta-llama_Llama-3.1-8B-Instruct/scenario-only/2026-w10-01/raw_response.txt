**Documentation**

**Intent:** This ontology design pattern models the semantic management of digital assets and the dynamic allocation of hardware resources for trustworthy AI, focusing on the characteristics and requirements of software and hardware entities.

**Covered requirements:**

* Strictly within the scenario scope
* Clear, self-explanatory class and property names
* Minimal but sufficient axioms (domain, range, subclass, disjointness)
* Declaration of every class with `a owl:Class`, every property with `a owl:ObjectProperty`, `a owl:DatatypeProperty`, or `a owl:AnnotationProperty`
* No creation of individual instances
* Addition of rdfs:label and rdfs:comment annotations to every class and property

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**

* The ontology assumes that the characteristics and requirements of software and hardware entities are described in a catalogue.
* The ontology excludes concepts not implied by the scenario, such as the actual allocation of hardware resources.
* The ontology does not consider the scalability and quantum-resilience aspects of the SMARTY research project.
