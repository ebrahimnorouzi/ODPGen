**Documentation**

**Intent:** This ontology models the relationships between tasks, prompting techniques, foundation models, and their associated constraints, resources, and usage patterns.

**Covered requirements:**

* Modeling tasks and their associated prompting techniques
* Describing data modalities and constraints involved in tasks
* Representing model architectures and their dependencies on resources
* Analyzing model usage and its association with tasks and foundation models
* Examining prior chat analysis and its relation to tasks and foundation models
* Investigating model evolution and its impact on tasks and foundation models

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**

* This ontology assumes that tasks are the primary entities of interest and that prompting techniques, foundation models, and their associated constraints and resources are secondary entities.
* It excludes modeling the specific details of foundation models, such as their architecture or training data, as these are not directly relevant to the scenario.
* It also excludes modeling the specific techniques used for prior chat analysis or model evolution, as these are not explicitly mentioned in the scenario.
