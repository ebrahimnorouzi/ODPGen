### 2. Scenario-requirement-to-axiom mapping

*   **"Mike needs to solve a task by prompting a foundation model."** -> `:Task`, `:Prompt`, `:FoundationModel`, `:targetsTask`, `:submittedToModel`.
*   **"Thus, he needs a prompting technique suitable for the task."** -> `:PromptingTechnique`, `:suitableForTask`, `:basedOnTechnique`.
*   **"Moreover, the task involves a specific data modality and is subject to different constraints."** -> `:DataModality`, `:Constraint`, `:involvesModality`, `:hasConstraint`.
*   **"Another constraint concerns the model architecture, which depends on the available resources."** -> `:ModelArchitecture`, `:Resource`, `:hasArchitecture`, `:dependsOnResource`.
*   **"He may also explore model families, variants, and evolutions..."** -> `:ModelFamily`, `:belongsToFamily`, `:hasVariant`, `:hasEvolution`.
*   **"...in order to experiment with prompts."** -> `:Prompt`.
*   **"Furthermore, he relies on the analysis of prior chats to design the solution."** -> `:Chat`, `:hasPrompt`.
*   **"Specifically, he can identify which models and prompting techniques are most frequently adopted for different tasks."** -> Supported structurally by paths connecting `:Chat` -> `:Prompt` -> `:FoundationModel` / `:PromptingTechnique` / `:Task`.
*   **"Regarding usage patterns, he may also be interested in statistics on conversation length..."** -> `:conversationLength` (DatatypeProperty).
*   **"...and in retrieving responses to specific prompts."** -> `:Response`, `:hasResponse`.

### 3. CQ-to-axiom mapping

*   **What are the tasks?** -> `:Task`
*   **What tasks exist for a given data modality?** -> `:Task`, `:involvesModality`, `:DataModality`
*   **What are the constraints of a given task?** -> `:Task`, `:hasConstraint`, `:Constraint`
*   **What are the possible foundation model architectures?** -> `:FoundationModel`, `:hasArchitecture`, `:ModelArchitecture`
*   **What are the variants and evolutions of a given foundation model?** -> `:FoundationModel`, `:hasVariant`, `:hasEvolution`
*   **What data modalities can a given foundation model process?** -> `:FoundationModel`, `:processesModality`, `:DataModality`
*   **What prompts are based on a given prompting technique?** -> `:Prompt`, `:basedOnTechnique`, `:PromptingTechnique`
*   **What is the response to a given prompt?** -> `:Prompt`, `:hasResponse`, `:Response`
*   **How many prompts and responses are there on average per chat?** -> `:Chat`, `:hasPrompt`, `:Prompt`, `:hasResponse`, `:Response` (Averages are calculated via SPARQL aggregate functions over these properties).
*   **Which foundation model is a given prompt submitted to?** -> `:Prompt`, `:submittedToModel`, `:FoundationModel`
*   **How many chats were conducted per model?** -> `:Chat`, `:hasPrompt`, `:Prompt`, `:submittedToModel`, `:FoundationModel` (Counted via SPARQL aggregate functions).
*   **Which prompts are based on a prompting technique that is considered suitable for the targeted task?** -> `:Prompt`, `:basedOnTechnique`, `:PromptingTechnique`, `:suitableForTask`, `:Task`, `:targetsTask`
*   **What are the prompting techniques?** -> `:PromptingTechnique`
*   **What prompting techniques are suitable for a given task?** -> `:PromptingTechnique`, `:suitableForTask`, `:Task`
*   **What prompting techniques are suitable for tasks targeting a given data modality?** -> `:PromptingTechnique`, `:suitableForTask`, `:Task`, `:involvesModality`, `:DataModality`
*   **What prompting techniques are suitable for tasks with a constraint on a given variable?** -> `:PromptingTechnique`, `:suitableForTask`, `:Task`, `:hasConstraint`, `:Constraint`, `:constrainsVariable`, `:Variable`

### 4. Intentionally excluded concepts

*   **Mike (User/Agent):** Excluded because the scenario uses "Mike" merely as a narrative device to introduce the requirements. None of the CQs require tracking users, agents, or ownership of the chats.
*   **Solution:** Excluded as an explicit class. In the context of the scenario, the "solution" is the successful execution of a `Task` via a `Prompt` and its `Response`. Creating a standalone `Solution` class would be redundant and is not queried in the CQs.
*   **Statistics / Frequency / Average:** Excluded as classes or properties. These are analytical operations (e.g., `COUNT`, `AVG`) performed at the query level (SPARQL) over the structural data, rather than ontological entities.
