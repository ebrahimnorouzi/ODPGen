### Documentation

**Intent:** 
This ontology design pattern models the domain of foundation model interaction, capturing the relationships between tasks, prompting techniques, model architectures, constraints, and the analysis of historical chat data to design effective solutions.

**Covered requirements:**
*   **Task Solving & Prompting:** Models `Task`, `Prompt`, and `FoundationModel` with properties like `solvesTask` and `promptsModel`.
*   **Prompting Techniques:** Includes `PromptingTechnique` and links it to tasks (`isSuitableForTask`) and prompts (`appliesTechnique`).
*   **Modalities & Constraints:** Captures `DataModality` and `Constraint` (including `ModelArchitectureConstraint`), linking them to tasks and architectures.
*   **Architecture & Resources:** Models `ModelArchitecture` and its dependency on `Resource` (`dependsOnResource`).
*   **Model Lineage:** Represents `ModelFamily` and allows exploration of variants and evolutions via `hasVariant` and `hasEvolution` properties between foundation models.
*   **Chat Analysis & Solution Design:** Introduces `User` (representing Mike), `Chat`, and `Solution`, enabling the user to analyze prior chats (`analyzesChat`) to design solutions (`designsSolution`).
*   **Adoption Tracking:** Uses `isAdoptedForTask` and `adoptionFrequency` to identify frequently used models and techniques for specific tasks.
*   **Usage Patterns & Statistics:** Captures `UsagePattern`, `Statistic`, and specific metrics like `conversationLength`, while allowing retrieval of responses to specific prompts (`hasResponse`).

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
*   **User Abstraction:** The specific individual "Mike" is abstracted into a general `User` class to maintain schema reusability.
*   **Variants and Evolutions:** Model variants and evolutions are modeled as recursive object properties (`hasVariant`, `hasEvolution`) on the `FoundationModel` class rather than separate classes, assuming they represent relative relationships between models.
*   **Adoption Frequency:** Modeled as a datatype property (`xsd:float`) to represent statistical frequency, assuming it is a quantifiable metric derived from chat analysis.
*   **Exclusions:** The ontology strictly avoids modeling the internal mechanics of the foundation models (e.g., weights, layers) or the specific content of the data modalities, as these are not mentioned in the scenario.
