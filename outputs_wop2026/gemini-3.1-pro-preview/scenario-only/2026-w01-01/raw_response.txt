### Documentation

**Intent:** 
To model the execution, inputs, outputs, and metadata of a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) study for a technological prototype, capturing the relationships between software tools, data sources, methodologies, and resulting datasets.

**Covered requirements:**
*   Modeling LCA studies within broader research projects.
*   Linking studies to evaluated prototypes and their underlying technologies.
*   Representing the software tools used and their connection to Bills of Materials (BoM) detailing electronic components.
*   Tracing inventory data to source databases, including capturing database versions.
*   Associating environmental impact calculations with specific methodologies.
*   Capturing the resulting outputs as distinct inventory and impact data.
*   Structuring study metadata into specific categories: hypotheses, parametrisation, system boundaries, and actor information.

**Known assumptions and exclusions:**
*   **Assumptions:** Inventory data and impact data are modeled as distinct, disjoint subclasses of a broader `LCAData` concept. Metadata elements (hypotheses, boundaries, etc.) are modeled as distinct object classes rather than simple literal values to allow for future expansion (e.g., linking a specific hypothesis to a specific calculation).
*   **Exclusions:** The pattern excludes the actual numerical data points, specific calculation formulas, or the internal structure of the LCAnet ontology network, focusing strictly on the structural relationships of the study described in the scenario.
